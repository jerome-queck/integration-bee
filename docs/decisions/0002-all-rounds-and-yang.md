# All rounds and the Yang guide

Both stay. Units 01 through 18 teach Yang Chapter 1 and Chapter 2. `sources/fetch.sh` extracts Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3 from `Resource_pack.zip` into `sources/past/r1`, `r2`, and `r3`. Round 2 in 2025 has `uk` and `sg`.

The PDFs are gitignored. `past/inventory.json` is the committed list. `verify/all_rounds_check.py` checks every PDF when they are on disk, and checks only the indexes when they are not.

A plaintext extraction of the Yang book is not committed. The PDF is.
