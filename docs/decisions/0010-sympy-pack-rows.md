# SymPy-gated pack rows

Resource-pack rows in Rounds 1–3 can be `verify: sympy` when a layout reading of the committed problem PDF and the cited mark scheme is proved by SymPy. The proof is a derivative for an indefinite integral, or a closed evaluation for a definite integral. A closed evaluation may use a floor sum from 0 to infinity when the integrand is constant on each integer interval, the even part on symmetric limits, King's reflection, a constant sum `f(x)+f(b-x)` on `[0, b]`, the reciprocal substitution on `(0, ∞)`, positive assumptions on parameters other than `x`, or `x = exp(-u)` on `[0, 1]` when the integrand contains a logarithm. Further checked reductions are an inverse-trig complement on a positive argument, including an even power, a reciprocal substitution on `[a, 1/a]`, a logarithm on `(0, ∞)` folded to an even integral, a hyperbolic function rewritten as a rational function, a multiple-angle form of a rational function of sine and cosine, and `sqrt(1±sin)` or `sqrt(1±cos)` rewritten as an absolute value. A checked double-angle equation evaluates `log(sin)` on `[0, π/2]`. That value is reused for `log(1+x²)/(1+x²)` after `x = tan θ` and for `x cot x` after integration by parts. Other checked forms are a reflection whose factors multiply to a constant, a square-root substitution that leaves an inverse-tangent complement, a power of tangent that averages to `1/2`, a parameter derivative reduced by the tangent substitution, the split of `log(x+1/x)/(x+1/x)`, the Taylor series of `log(1-x)` with absolutely convergent term integrals, integration by parts down to a beta integral, an explicit arcsine antiderivative, the Fourier series of the real part of `exp(c e^{ix})`, and the substitution `u = sin x - cos x` for `cos x/(2 - sin 2x)` on `[0, π/2]`. A one-letter dummy other than `x` is stored as `x` when `x` is not already a parameter. The letter `i` in a layout reading is the imaginary unit. A chain of equalities in a mark scheme is kept only when every link is an identity, and a factor of the constant of integration is not cleared when it still depends on `x`. `log|u|` differentiates as `log(u)` on each interval where `u` keeps its sign. Further checked forms are a constant sum of reciprocal square roots of logarithms on a finite interval, the beta evaluation of a power of tangent differentiated at the exponent 0, a cosine modulus through the half-angle substitution, the geometric series for `x^n/(e^x-1)` and the split that turns `x^n/(e^x+1)` into that series, a shift of `log Γ`, a sine weight on `log Γ`, a product of sines reduced to a damped cosine difference, `cot(x) log(sec x)` by the series for `log(1-v)`, the derivative of a polylog in `x^x`, `log(1+tan x)` on `[0, π/4]`, the odd-zeta series for `log(t)/(1-t^2)`, the damped integral of `(t-sin t)/t^2`, a sine-square substitution, the Wallis product, an artanh series reduced to the Wallis integral, and a Chebyshev polynomial reduction. A reciprocal power that clears to a rational function is integrated directly, and `(cos(a x)-cos(b x))/x^2` on `[0, ∞)` reduces by parts to the Dirichlet integral for positive `a` and `b`. The layout reader also accepts ceiling brackets, drops a lone centered digit in the bottom margin, and drops a glyph that sits entirely above the problem number. Further checked forms are a complex average, a Gaussian cosine transform, a sine substitution into a quadratic, a sech transform on a rectangular contour, and a positive log-cosine parameter integral. Two literal infinite forms are checked before the closed-expression gate: a telescoping cosine product and a geometric series with a reflected 2024th power. Numeric agreement is not a proof.

`scripts/recover_pack_math.py` is the only writer of those fields. It rereads the committed Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3 PDFs and leaves every unproved row `manual` or `unchecked`. Yang practice is not gated. The former manual example `R1-2024-Q01` is now proved by SymPy, with the same mark-scheme citation containing `sin(1)`. The manual-mark fixture uses `R1-2023-Q05`.

`make check` re-proves the stored expressions and does not open the PDFs. A mark of a `sympy` row sends `extract.final_answer` and does not send `verdict`. A manual row still needs a verdict. An unchecked row still has no answer.
