## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty x^3 e^{-x^2}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=x^2\), so \(x\,dx=du/2\). The integrand \(x^3 e^{-x^2}\,dx\) becomes \(\frac12 u e^{-u}\,du\), and
\[
\frac12\int_0^\infty u e^{-u}\,du=\frac12\Gamma(2)=\frac12.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(1/2\).

## Final answer

\frac12.
