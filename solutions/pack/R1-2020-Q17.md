## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\ln(1+x)}{x\sqrt{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Integrate by parts with \(u=\ln(1+x)\) and \(dv=x^{-3/2}\,dx\). The boundary term \(-2\ln(1+x)/\sqrt{x}\) vanishes at both ends, and
\[
2\int_0^\infty\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}(1+x)}=4\arctan\sqrt{x}\Big|_0^\infty=2\pi.
\]
The answers sheet prints \(2\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
2\pi.
