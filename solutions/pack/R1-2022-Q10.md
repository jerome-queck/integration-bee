## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Write \(1/(e^x+1)=e^{-x}/(1+e^{-x})\) and expand the geometric series for \(x>0\):
\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x+1}\,dx=\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n\int_0^\infty x^3 e^{-(n+1)x}\,dx=\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n\frac{\Gamma(4)}{(n+1)^4}.
\]
Then \(\Gamma(4)=6\) and the alternating zeta value is the Dirichlet eta function \(\eta(4)=(1-2^{1-4})\zeta(4)\). With \(\zeta(4)=\pi^4/90\),
\[
6\eta(4)=\frac{7\pi^4}{120}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(7\pi^4/120\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{7\pi^4}{120}.
\]
