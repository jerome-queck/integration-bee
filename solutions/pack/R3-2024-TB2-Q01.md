## Problem

\[\int\frac{e^{e^{x}}+1}{e^{e^{x}-x}+1}\,dx.\]

## Solution

Differentiate \(\ln(e^{e^{x}}+e^{x})\): \[\frac{e^{e^{x}}e^{x}+e^{x}}{e^{e^{x}}+e^{x}}=\frac{e^{x}(e^{e^{x}}+1)}{e^{e^{x}}+e^{x}}.\]

The denominator of the integrand is \(e^{e^{x}}/e^{x}+1=(e^{e^{x}}+e^{x})/e^{x}\), so the integrand is the same derivative. The tiebreaker sheet prints this antiderivative.
