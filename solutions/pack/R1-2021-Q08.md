## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{1}{\bigl(x^3+x^{-3}\bigr)^2}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The integrand equals \(x^6/(x^6+1)^2\) and is even, so the integral is \(2\int_0^\infty x^6/(x^6+1)^2\,dx\). Set \(u=x^6\). Then
\[
\frac13\int_0^\infty\frac{u^{1/6}}{(u+1)^2}\,du=\frac13 B\Bigl(\frac76,\frac56\Bigr).
\]
The Gamma recurrence \(\Gamma(7/6)=\Gamma(1/6)/6\) and the reflection formula \(\Gamma(1/6)\Gamma(5/6)=\pi/\sin(\pi/6)=2\pi\) give
\[
\frac1{18}\Gamma\Bigl(\frac16\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\frac56\Bigr)=\frac\pi9.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/9\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac\pi9.
\]
