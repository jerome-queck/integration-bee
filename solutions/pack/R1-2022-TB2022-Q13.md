## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\sqrt{-\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(x=e^{-u}\). The integral becomes
\[
\int_0^{\infty}u^{1/2}e^{-u}\,du=\Gamma\Bigl(\frac32\Bigr)=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\sqrt{\pi}/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}.
\]
