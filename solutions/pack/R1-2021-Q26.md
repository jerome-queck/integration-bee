## Problem

\[
\int_1^e\frac{x-x\ln x+1}{x(x+1)^2+x\ln^2 x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Differentiate \(\arctan\bigl(\ln x/(1+x)\bigr)\). With \(f=\ln x/(1+x)\),

\[
f'=\frac{1+x-x\ln x}{x(1+x)^2},\qquad
\frac{f'}{1+f^2}=\frac{x-x\ln x+1}{x(x+1)^2+x\ln^2 x}.
\]

The definite integral is therefore

\[
\arctan\Bigl(\frac{\ln x}{1+x}\Bigr)\Big|_1^e=\arctan\Bigl(\frac{1}{1+e}\Bigr).
\]

For a positive argument, \(\arctan(1/y)=\operatorname{arccot} y\). The mark scheme prints \(\operatorname{arccot}(1+e)\).

## Final answer

\[
\operatorname{arccot}(1+e).
\]
