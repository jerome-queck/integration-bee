## Problem

\[
\int\frac{1}{x^{23}+x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF treats the general integral
\[
\int\frac{dx}{x^{n}+x}=\int\frac{x^{-n}}{1+x^{1-n}}\,dx=\frac{1}{1-n}\ln|1+x^{1-n}|+C.
\]
For \(n=23\),
\[
\frac{1}{1-23}\ln|1+x^{-22}|=-\frac{1}{22}\ln\left|\frac{x^{22}+1}{x^{22}}\right|=\ln|x|-\frac{1}{22}\ln(x^{22}+1)+C.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\ln x-(1/22)\ln(x^{22}+1)+C\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln|x|-\dfrac{1}{22}\ln(x^{22}+1)+C.
\]
