## Problem

\[
\int\sqrt{x-\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x-\sqrt{x+\cdots}}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The nested radical is the limit of the alternating iteration. That iteration converges for \(x>3/4\), and its limit is the closed form stored in the catalog, \(A=-1/2+\sqrt{4x-3}/2\). The gate proves the integral of that closed form.

The worked PDF sets

\[
A=\sqrt{x-\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x-\sqrt{x+\cdots}}}},\qquad
B=\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x-\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x-\cdots}}}},
\]

so \(A=\sqrt{x-B}\), \(B=\sqrt{x+A}\), and \(B^2-A^2=A+B\). For the positive branch \(B\neq -A\) this gives \(B-A=1\). Then \(A=\sqrt{x-(1+A)}\), and the quadratic \(A^2+A+1-x=0\) has positive root

\[
A=-\frac12+\frac12\sqrt{4x-3}
\]

for \(x>3/4\), where the nested radical converges. Integrating that closed form gives the answers-sheet antiderivative.

## Final answer

\[
\frac{(4x-3)^{3/2}}{12}-\frac{x}{2}+C.
\]
