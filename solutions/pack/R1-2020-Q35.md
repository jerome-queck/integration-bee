## Problem

\[
\int_0^\pi\arctan\bigl(3^{\cos x}\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The reflection \(x\mapsto\pi-x\) replaces \(\cos x\) by \(-\cos x\) and leaves the integral unchanged. For every positive \(y\), \(\arctan y+\arctan(1/y)=\pi/2\), so
\[
\arctan\bigl(3^{\cos x}\bigr)+\arctan\bigl(3^{-\cos x}\bigr)=\frac\pi2.
\]
Averaging the two copies gives
\[
\frac12\int_0^\pi\frac\pi2\,dx=\frac{\pi^2}{4}.
\]
The answers sheet prints \(\pi^2/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi^2}{4}.
\]
