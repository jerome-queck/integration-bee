## Problem

\[
\int_3^5\ln\Gamma(x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Raabe's formula says \(\int_n^{n+1}\ln\Gamma(x)\,dx=\frac12\ln(2\pi)+n\ln n-n\). Adding the cases \(n=3\) and \(n=4\) gives
\[
\ln(2\pi)+3\ln 3+4\ln 4-7=\ln(2\pi)+3\ln 3+8\ln 2-7.
\]
Since \(13824=24^3=2^9\cdot 3^3\), this equals \(\ln(13824\pi)-7\). The mark scheme prints \(\ln(13824\pi)-7\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln(13824\pi)-7.
\]
