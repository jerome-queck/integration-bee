## Problem

\[
\int_1^{2024}\frac{e^{x^x}}{\dfrac1x\cdot\dfrac1x\cdot\dfrac2x\cdot\dfrac3x\cdot\dfrac5x\cdot\dfrac8x\cdot\dfrac{13}x\cdot\dfrac{21}x\cdots}\,dx.

## Solution

The denominator is an infinite product of Fibonacci numbers over \(x\). The Fibonacci sequence is unbounded, so for each fixed \(x>0\) the factors \(F_k/x\) are eventually larger than \(2\). The product diverges to \(+\infty\), and the integrand is \(0\) at every point of \([1,2024]\). The integral of the zero function is \(0\). The worked PDF gives the same reason: the denominator is infinite. The mark scheme prints \(0\).
