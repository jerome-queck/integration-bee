## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^{2}+4x+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Complete the square in the exponent:
\[
-x^{2}+4x+1=-(x-2)^{2}+5.
\]
The worked PDF therefore factors out \(e^{5}\) and translates the Gaussian:
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^{2}+4x+1}\,dx=e^{5}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-(x-2)^{2}}\,dx=e^{5}\sqrt{\pi}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(e^{5}\sqrt{\pi}\).

## Final answer

\[
e^{5}\sqrt{\pi}.
\]
