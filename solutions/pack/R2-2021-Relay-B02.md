## Problem

\[
\int_{a}^{\infty}\ln\Bigl(1+\frac{169}{x^{2}}\Bigr)\,dx,
\]

## Solution

with the value passed on equal to the second digit of the integral. Shuttle B1 passes \(a=0\).

The antiderivative is \(x\ln(1+169/x^{2})+26\arctan(x/13)\). The boundary term \(x\ln(1+169/x^{2})\) vanishes at both \(0\) and \(\infty\), and \(26\cdot\pi/2=13\pi\). Numerically \(13\pi=40.84\ldots\), so the integer part is \(40\) and the second digit is \(0\). The mark scheme prints \(0\).
