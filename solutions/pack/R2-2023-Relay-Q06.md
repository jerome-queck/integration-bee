## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{2\pi}\frac{\sin^{2}(5x)\sin^{2}(4x)}{\sin^{2}x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The quotient of sines is the Dirichlet polynomial
\[
\frac{\sin(5x)}{\sin x}=16\cos^{4}x-12\cos^{2}x+1,
\]
with removable singularities at multiples of \(\pi\). The integrand is that square times \(\sin^{2}(4x)\). Expanding in exponentials and integrating term by term over a full period gives \(4\pi\). The mark scheme prints \(4\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
4\pi.
\]
