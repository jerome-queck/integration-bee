## Problem

\[
\int_{-r}^{r}\max\bigl(\sqrt{r^{2}-x^{2}},|x|\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The graphs meet at \(|x|=r/\sqrt{2}\). Inside that point the semicircle is larger, and outside it the absolute value is larger. The semicircle antiderivative is \(\tfrac12\bigl(x\sqrt{r^{2}-x^{2}}+r^{2}\arcsin(x/r)\bigr)\). Its value at \(r/\sqrt{2}\) is \(r^{2}/4+\pi r^{2}/8\). Doubling and adding
\[
2\int_{r/\sqrt{2}}^{r}x\,dx=\frac{r^{2}}{2}
\]
gives \(r^{2}(1+\pi/4)\). The mark scheme prints \((1+\pi/4)r^{2}\).

## Final answer

\[
\Bigl(1+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)r^{2}.
\]
