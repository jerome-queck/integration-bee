## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\ln x\ln(1-x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Expand \(\ln(1-x)=-\sum_{n\ge 1}x^{n}/n\). Then
\[
\int_0^1 x^{n}\ln x\,dx=-\frac{1}{(n+1)^{2}},
\]
and the integral equals
\[
\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{1}{n(n+1)^{2}}.
\]
The partial fractions \(1/(n(n+1)^{2})=1/n-1/(n+1)-1/(n+1)^{2}\) sum to \(1-(\pi^{2}/6-1)=2-\pi^{2}/6\). The mark scheme prints \(2-\pi^{2}/6\).

## Final answer

\[
2-\dfrac{\pi^{2}}{6}.
\]
