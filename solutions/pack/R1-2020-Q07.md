## Problem

\[
\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}\left(\sqrt[3]{x}+1\right)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=x^{1/6}\), so \(x=u^{6}\) and \(dx=6u^{5}\,du\). Then \(x^{-1/2}=u^{-3}\) and \(x^{1/3}=u^{2}\), and the integrand becomes
\[
\frac{u^{-3}}{1+u^{2}}\cdot 6u^{5}\,du=6\frac{u^{2}}{1+u^{2}}\,du=6\left(1-\frac{1}{1+u^{2}}\right)du.
\]
Integrating gives \(6u-6\arctan u+C\). The answers sheet prints \(6x^{1/6}-6\arctan(x^{1/6})+C\).

## Final answer

\[
6x^{1/6}-6\arctan(x^{1/6})+C.
\]
