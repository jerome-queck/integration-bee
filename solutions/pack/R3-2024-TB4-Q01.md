## Problem

\[\int_{-2024}^{2025}\frac{1}{3^{x}+\sqrt{3}}\,dx.\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=1/(3^{x}+\sqrt{3})\). Then \(f(x)+f(1-x)=1/\sqrt{3}\). The map \(x\mapsto 1-x\) preserves \([-2024,2025]\), an interval of length \(4049\). Adding the integral to its reflected copy gives \[2I=\int_{-2024}^{2025}\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,dx=\frac{4049}{\sqrt{3}},\]

so \(I=4049/(2\sqrt{3})\). The tiebreaker sheet prints that value.

## Final answer

\[\dfrac{4049}{2\sqrt{3}}.\]
