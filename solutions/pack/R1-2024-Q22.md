## Problem

\[
\int_a^b\frac{\arctan(x/a)+\arctan(x/b)}{x}\,dx,\qquad a>0,\; b>0.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(y=ab/x\) swaps the two arctangents and preserves the measure \(dx/x\). Adding the original integral \(I\) to itself and using \(\arctan u+\arctan(1/u)=\pi/2\) for \(u>0\),

\[
2I=\int_a^b\frac{\pi}{x}\,dx=\pi\ln\frac b a.
\]

Hence \(I=(\pi/2)\ln(b/a)\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac\pi2\ln\frac b a.
\]
