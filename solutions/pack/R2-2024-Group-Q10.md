## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{1+x}\log\Bigl(\frac{1+3x}{2-x}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]
On this interval the sheet's \(\log\) is the natural logarithm.

## Solution

Let \(f(t)=\ln(1+t)\) and \(g(x)=2x/(1+x)\). Then \(g\) is an increasing bijection of \([0,1]\) onto itself, with inverse \(g^{-1}(x)=x/(2-x)\), and
\[
f(g(x))+f(g^{-1}(x))=\ln\Bigl(\frac{1+3x}{2-x}\Bigr)+\ln\Bigl(\frac{2}{1+x}\Bigr).
\]
The general identity
\[
\int_{a}^{b}\bigl(f(g(x))+f(g^{-1}(x))\bigr)f'(x)\,dx=f(b)^{2}-f(a)^{2}
\]
with \(f'(x)=1/(1+x)\) gives
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{1+x}\ln\Bigl(\frac{1+3x}{2-x}\Bigr)\,dx+\int_{0}^{1}\frac{1}{1+x}\ln\Bigl(\frac{2}{1+x}\Bigr)\,dx=(\ln 2)^{2}.
\]
The second integral equals \((\ln 2)^{2}/2\), so the first is the same value. The mark scheme prints \(\tfrac12(\ln 2)^{2}\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{2}(\ln 2)^{2}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy checks \((1+g(x))(1+g^{-1}(x))=(1+3x)/(2-x)\cdot 2/(1+x)\) and \(g(g^{-1}(x))=x\), and evaluates
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\ln(2/(1+x))}{1+x}\,dx=\frac{(\ln 2)^{2}}{2}.
\]
