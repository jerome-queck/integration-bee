## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\frac{dx}{1+\lfloor 1/x\rfloor}.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(t=1/x\) from the worked PDF gives

\[
\int_1^\infty\frac{dt}{t^2(1+\lfloor t\rfloor)}=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n+1}\int_n^{n+1}t^{-2}\,dt
=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\Bigl(\frac1n-\frac{1}{n+1}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^2}\Bigr).
\]

The first two terms telescope to \(1\), and \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty 1/(n+1)^2=\pi^2/6-1\), so the sum is \(2-\pi^2/6\).

## Final answer

\[
2-\frac{\pi^2}{6}.
\]
