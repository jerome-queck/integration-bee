## Problem

\[
\int\frac{dx}{\csc x+1}.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF rationalises the integrand:

\[
\frac{1}{\csc x+1}=\frac{\sin x}{1+\sin x}=\tan x\sec x-\tan^2 x=\tan x\sec x-\sec^2 x+1.
\]

Integrating term by term gives \(x+\sec x-\tan x+C\). The mark scheme prints

\[
x-\frac{2\sin(x/2)}{\sin(x/2)+\cos(x/2)}+C.
\]

These differ by the constant \(1\), because

\[
\sec x-\tan x=\frac{1-\sin x}{\cos x}=1-\frac{2\sin(x/2)}{\sin(x/2)+\cos(x/2)}.
\]

Both are antiderivatives of the integrand. The worked PDF's expression is the one obtained by the steps above.

## Final answer

\[
x+\sec x-\tan x+C.
\]
