## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\arctan(x^{n})}{x}\,dx,\qquad n\ge 1\text{ an integer}.
\]

## Solution

The solutions PDF expands \(\arctan z=\sum_{k\ge 0}(-1)^{k}z^{2k+1}/(2k+1)\) and integrates term by term:
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\arctan(x^{n})}{x}\,dx=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{(2k+1)^{2}n}=\frac{G}{n},
\]
where \(G\) is the Catalan constant. The same value follows from \(y=x^{n}\). The mark scheme prints \(G/n\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{G}{n}.
\]
