## Problem

\[
\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\frac{\cos x}{1+e^{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=\cos x/(1+e^{x})\). The substitution \(u=-x\) in the worked PDF gives
\[
f(x)+f(-x)=\cos x,
\]
because \(f(-x)=\cos x\cdot e^{x}/(1+e^{x})\). Therefore
\[
2I=\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx=\sin x\Big|_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}=2,
\]
so \(I=1\). The answers sheet prints \(1\).

1.
