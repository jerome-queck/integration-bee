## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2} x\prod_{i=1}^\infty\cos\Bigl(\frac{x}{2^i}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF starts from \(\cos x=\sin(2x)/(2\sin x)\) and writes the partial product. Iterating that identity gives

\[
\sin x=2^n\sin\Bigl(\frac{x}{2^n}\Bigr)\prod_{i=1}^n\cos\Bigl(\frac{x}{2^i}\Bigr).
\]

As \(n\to\infty\), \(2^n\sin(x/2^n)\to x\), so

\[
x\prod_{i=1}^\infty\cos\Bigl(\frac{x}{2^i}\Bigr)=\sin x
\]

for \(x\in(0,\pi)\). The worked PDF stops after the finite product. The mark scheme prints the value of the finished integral,

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin x\,dx=1.
\]
