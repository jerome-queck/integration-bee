## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\bigl(x+1/x\bigr)^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(x\neq 0\),
\[
\frac{1}{(x+1/x)^{2}}=\frac{x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}=\frac{1}{x^{2}+1}-\frac{1}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}.
\]
The singularity at \(0\) is removable. The two integrals over \(\mathbb{R}\) are \(\pi\) and \(\pi/2\), so the difference is \(\pi/2\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{2}.
\]
