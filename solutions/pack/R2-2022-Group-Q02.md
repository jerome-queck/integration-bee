## Problem

\[
\int\frac{x^{n}}{1+x+\dfrac{x^{2}}{2!}+\cdots+\dfrac{x^{n}}{n!}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let
\[
p(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}.
\]
Term by term, \(p'(x)=p(x)-x^{n}/n!\). Therefore
\[
\frac{x^{n}}{p(x)}=n!\bigl(1-(\ln p(x))'\bigr),
\]
and an antiderivative is
\[
n!\,x-n!\ln p(x)+C.
\]
The mark scheme prints that expression.

## Final answer

\[
n!\,x-n!\ln\Biggl(1+x+\frac{x^{2}}{2!}+\cdots+\frac{x^{n}}{n!}\Biggr)+C.
\]
