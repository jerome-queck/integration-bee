## Problem

\[
\int_1^\infty\Bigl(\frac{\ln x}{x}\Bigr)^{2011}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=1/x\). Then \(dx=-du/u^2\) and the limits run from \(1\) to \(0\). The two minus signs, one from \(dx\) and one from reversing the limits, give

\[
\int_0^1(-\ln u)^{2011}u^{2009}\,du.
\]

Set \(t=-\ln u\). The integral becomes

\[
\int_0^\infty t^{2011}e^{-2010 t}\,dt=\frac{\Gamma(2012)}{2010^{2012}}=\frac{2011!}{2010^{2012}}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{2011!}{2010^{2012}}.
\]
