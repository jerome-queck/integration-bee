## Problem

\[
\int_0^{1}\frac{\sin(\ln x)}{\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF writes \(\sin(\ln x)=(x^{i}-x^{-i})/(2i)\) and introduces the parameter integral \(\int_0^{1}x^{a}\,dx=1/(a+1)\):
\[
\int_0^{1}\frac{\sin(\ln x)}{\ln x}\,dx=\frac{1}{2i}\int_{-i}^{i}\frac{da}{1+a}=\frac{1}{2i}\ln\frac{1+i}{1-i}.
\]
The quotient \((1+i)/(1-i)\) equals \(i\), and \(\ln i=i\pi/2\) on the principal branch, so the value is \(\pi/4\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{4}.
\]
