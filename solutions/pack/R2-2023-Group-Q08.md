## Problem

\[
\int\sqrt{\bigl(\sin 23x+10\sin 24x+\sin 25x\bigr)^{2}+\bigl(\cos 23x+10\cos 24x+\cos 25x\bigr)^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(A=\sin 23x+10\sin 24x+\sin 25x\) and \(B\) the cosine analogue. Then
\[
(\sin 23x+\sin 25x)^{2}+(\cos 23x+\cos 25x)^{2}=2+2\cos 2x,
\]
and the cross terms with the angle \(24x\) contribute \(20(\cos x+\cos x)=40\cos x\). Adding the \(10^{2}\) from the middle terms,
\[
A^{2}+B^{2}=(10+2\cos x)^{2}.
\]
On the range where \(10+2\cos x>0\), the square root is \(10+2\cos x\). An antiderivative is \(10x+2\sin x\). The mark scheme prints \(10x+2\sin x\).

## Final answer

\[
10x+2\sin x.
\]
