## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-c(y+1/y)}y^{-1/2}\,dy
\]

## Solution

for \(c>0\). Set \(y=t^{2}\). The integral becomes
\[
2\int_{0}^{\infty}\exp\bigl(-c(t^{2}+t^{-2})\bigr)\,dt=2e^{-2c}\int_{0}^{\infty}\exp\bigl(-c(t-1/t)^{2}\bigr)\,dt.
\]
The standard Gaussian evaluation
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\exp(-ct^{2}-c/t^{2})\,dt=\frac12\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{c}}\,e^{-2c}
\]
then gives \(\sqrt{\pi/c}\,e^{-2c}\). The mark scheme prints \(\sqrt{\pi/c}\,e^{-2c}\).

## Final answer

\[
\sqrt{\dfrac{\pi}{c}}\,e^{-2c}.
\]
