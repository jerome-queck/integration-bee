## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln\bigl(7997\sin^{2}\theta+7945\cos^{2}\theta\bigr)\,d\theta.
\]

## Solution

The solutions PDF compares the integrand with \(\ln(1+r^{2}-2r\cos x)\) and evaluates that parameter integral. The same algebra starts from
\[
\alpha\sin^{2}\theta+\beta\cos^{2}\theta=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}+\frac{\beta-\alpha}{2}\cos 2\theta
\]
with \(\alpha=7997\) and \(\beta=7945\). The constant term that remains after removing the cosine factor is \((\sqrt{\alpha}+\sqrt{\beta})^{2}/4\), and half of the full-period logarithm produces
\[
\pi\ln\Bigl(\frac{\sqrt{7997}+\sqrt{7945}}{2}\Bigr).
\]
The mark scheme prints that value.

## Final answer

\[
\pi\ln\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt{7997}+\sqrt{7945}}{2}\Bigr).
\]
