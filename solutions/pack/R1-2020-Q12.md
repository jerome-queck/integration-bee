## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin^2 x}{x^2(x^2+1)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF differentiates under the integral sign. For
\[
I(a)=\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin^2(ax)}{x^2(x^2+1)}\,dx,
\]
three differentiations produce the equation \(I'''(a)=-2\pi+4I'(a)\). The initial values \(I'(0)=0\) and \(I''(0)=\pi\) select
\[
I'(a)=\frac\pi2\bigl(1-e^{-2a}\bigr).
\]
Then \(I(0)=0\), so
\[
I(1)=\int_0^1 I'(a)\,da=\frac\pi4+\frac\pi{4e^2}.
\]
The answers sheet prints \(\pi/4+\pi/(4e^2)\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac\pi4+\frac\pi{4e^2}.
\]
