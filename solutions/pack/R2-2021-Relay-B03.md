## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\ln(b(x+1))}{x^{2}+1}\,dx,
\]

## Solution

and pass that value on. Shuttle B2 passes \(b=0\).

Split the integrand as \(\ln b+\ln(x+1)\). Then
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{dx}{x^{2}+1}=\frac{\pi}{4},
\]
while \(\ln(x+1)/(x^{2}+1)\) is continuous on \([0,1]\). The factor \(\ln b\) tends to \(-\infty\) as \(b\to 0^{+}\), so the integral is \(-\infty\). The mark scheme prints \(-\infty\).
