## Problem

\[
\int_1^\infty\frac{e^{\operatorname{arcsec}\sqrt{x}}}{x\sqrt{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=\operatorname{arcsec}\sqrt{x}\), so \(\sqrt{x}=\sec u\), \(x=\sec^2 u\), and \(dx=2\sec^2 u\tan u\,du\). The limits run from \(0\) to \(\pi/2\). Then

\[
\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{x}}=\frac{2\sec^2 u\tan u}{\sec^3 u}\,du=2\sin u\,du,
\]

and the integral is

\[
2\int_0^{\pi/2}e^u\sin u\,du=\Bigl[e^u(\sin u-\cos u)\Bigr]_0^{\pi/2}=e^{\pi/2}+1.
\]

One intermediate line of the worked PDF writes \(e^{-u}\) before reaching this antiderivative. The substitution above produces \(e^{u}\), and that is the integrand whose evaluation matches the mark scheme.

## Final answer

\[
e^{\pi/2}+1.
\]
