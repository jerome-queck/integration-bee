## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x+\sin(1/x)}{x(1+x^2)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Split the integrand. In the second term set \(u=1/x\):

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin(1/x)}{x(1+x^2)}\,dx=\int_0^\infty\frac{u\sin u}{1+u^2}\,du.
\]

Adding the first term,

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x}{x(1+x^2)}\,dx+\int_0^\infty\frac{x\sin x}{1+x^2}\,dx
=\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx=\frac\pi2,
\]

because \(1/(1+x^2)+x^2/(1+x^2)=1\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac\pi2.
\]
