## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\arctan(b\sin x)}{\sin x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Differentiate in the parameter \(b>0\):
\[
I'(b)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{1}{1+b^2\sin^2 x}\,dx.
\]
Write the denominator as \((1+b^2)\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x\) and substitute \(u=\tan x\). The integral collapses to
\[
I'(b)=\int_0^\infty\frac{du}{(1+b^2)u^2+1}=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{1+b^2}}.
\]
Since \(I(0)=0\),
\[
I(b)=\frac\pi2\int_0^b\frac{dt}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}=\frac\pi2\operatorname{arsinh} b.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi\operatorname{arsinh}(b)/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac\pi2\operatorname{arsinh} b.
\]
