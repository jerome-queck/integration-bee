## Problem

\[
\int\left(\frac{1}{1-\sin x}+\frac{1}{1+\sin x}\right)dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF adds the two fractions:
\[
\frac{1}{1-\sin x}+\frac{1}{1+\sin x}=\frac{2}{1-\sin^{2}x}=\frac{2}{\cos^{2}x}=2\sec^{2}x.
\]
Integrating gives \(2\tan x+C\). The mark scheme prints \(2\tan x+C\).

## Final answer

\[
2\tan x+C.
\]
