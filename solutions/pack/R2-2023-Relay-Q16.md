## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\lfloor x\rfloor\,e^{-x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On each interval \([n,n+1)\) the floor equals \(n\), so
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\lfloor x\rfloor\,e^{-x}\,dx=\sum_{n\ge 1}n\bigl(e^{-n}-e^{-(n+1)}\bigr)=(1-e^{-1})\sum_{n\ge 1}n\,e^{-n}.
\]
The arithmetico-geometric series \(\sum_{n\ge 1}nr^{n}=r/(1-r)^{2}\) at \(r=e^{-1}\) gives
\[
(1-e^{-1})\cdot\frac{e^{-1}}{(1-e^{-1})^{2}}=\frac{1}{e-1}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{e-1}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy sums \(\sum_{n\ge 1}n e^{-n}=e^{-1}/(1-e^{-1})^{2}\), and simplifies \((1-e^{-1})\) times that sum to \(1/(e-1)\).
