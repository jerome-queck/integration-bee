## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\sin(\cos^{-1}x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \([0,1]\), \(\sin(\cos^{-1}x)=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\). The integral is the area of a quarter disk,
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{4}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{4}.
\]
