## Problem

\[
\int_{1_{\mathbb{Q}}(e+\pi)}^{1_{\mathbb{Q}}(e\pi)}\cos(\pi x)\,dx,
\]

where \(1_{\mathbb{Q}}(t)=1\) if \(t\) is rational and \(0\) otherwise.

## Solution

The worked PDF recalls that \(e+\pi\) and \(e\pi\) are not both rational, and that it is not known from that fact alone which of the two endpoints is \(0\) or \(1\). The four formal possibilities are a zero-length interval, or an integral of \(\cos(\pi x)\) from \(0\) to \(1\), or the negative of that integral. In every case the value is \(0\), because

\[
\int_0^1\cos(\pi x)\,dx=0.
\]

## Final answer

\[
0.
\]

## SymPy self-check

The gate retains both original rationality predicates as `Contains` conditions
in binary `Piecewise` bounds. SymPy evaluates the antiderivative
\(\sin(\pi x)/\pi\) at these bounds as zero. No choice of either unresolved
rationality predicate is made. The other three binary endpoint pairs also give zero.
