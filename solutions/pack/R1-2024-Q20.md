## Problem

\[
\int_0^{100} x^{\{x\}-1}\bigl(\ln(x^x)+\{x\}\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On each interval \([n,n+1]\) write \(x=n+t\) with \(t\in[0,1]\). The integrand is the derivative of \((n+t)^t\):

\[
\frac{d}{dt}(n+t)^t=(n+t)^{t-1}\bigl((n+t)\ln(n+t)+t\bigr).
\]

Integrating from \(0\) to \(1\) gives \((n+1)^1-n^0=n\). Summing \(n\) from \(0\) to \(99\),

\[
\sum_{n=0}^{99}n=\frac{99\cdot 100}{2}=4950.
\]

At \(n=0\) the endpoint value \(t^t\) tends to \(1\) as \(t\to 0^+\), so that piece contributes \(0\).

## Final answer

\[
4950.
\]
