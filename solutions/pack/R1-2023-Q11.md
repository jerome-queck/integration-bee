## Problem

\[
\int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/3}\sec x\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The antiderivative is \(\ln|\sec x+\tan x|\). The worked PDF evaluates the endpoints:
\[
\ln(\sec(\pi/3)+\tan(\pi/3))-\ln(\sec(\pi/6)+\tan(\pi/6))
=\ln(2+\sqrt{3})-\ln\sqrt{3}
=\ln\left(1+\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right).
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\ln(2/\sqrt{3}+1)\), the same value.

## Final answer

\[
\ln\left(1+\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right).
\]
