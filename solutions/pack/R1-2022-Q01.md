## Problem

\[
\int_0^{1}\frac{1}{\sqrt{x-x^{2}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Complete the square: \(x-x^{2}=\frac14-(x-\frac12)^{2}\). The worked PDF integrates the resulting arcsine:
\[
\int_0^{1}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{\tfrac14-(x-\tfrac12)^{2}}}=\arcsin(2x-1)\Big|_0^{1}=\frac\pi2-\left(-\frac\pi2\right)=\pi.
\]
The same value is the beta integral \(B(1/2,1/2)=\Gamma(1/2)^{2}=\pi\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi.
