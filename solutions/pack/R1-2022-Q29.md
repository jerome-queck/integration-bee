## Problem

\[
\int_0^{1/2}\ln\bigl(\sqrt{1-x}-\sqrt{x}\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(x=\sin^{2}\theta\), so \(\theta\) runs from \(0\) to \(\pi/4\) and \(dx=\sin 2\theta\,d\theta\). The integral becomes
\[
\int_0^{\pi/4}\ln(\cos\theta-\sin\theta)\sin 2\theta\,d\theta.
\]
Integration by parts, with the boundary term \(\cos 2\theta\ln(\cos\theta-\sin\theta)\) vanishing at both ends, leaves
\[
-\int_0^{\pi/4}\frac{\cos 2\theta\,(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)}{2(\cos\theta-\sin\theta)}\,d\theta.
\]
The rational factor simplifies to \((1+\sin 2\theta)/2\), so the value is
\[
-\int_0^{\pi/4}\frac{1+\sin 2\theta}{2}\,d\theta=-\frac{\pi+2}{8}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(-(\pi+2)/8\), which is \(-\pi/8-1/4\).

## Final answer

\[
-\dfrac{\pi+2}{8}.
\]
