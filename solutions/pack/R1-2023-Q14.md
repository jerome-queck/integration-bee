## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\bigl(\ln\ln x\bigr)^{\ln(\ln x)/\ln(\ln\ln x)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \((0,1)\) the logarithms of logarithms are not all real. The worked PDF rewrites the displayed power formally in base \(e\):

\[
\bigl(\ln\ln x\bigr)^{\ln(\ln x)/\ln(\ln\ln x)}
=\exp\Biggl(\ln(\ln\ln x)\cdot\frac{\ln(\ln x)}{\ln(\ln\ln x)}\Biggr)
=\ln x.
\]

The integral is \(\int_0^1\ln x\,dx\). The antiderivative \(x\ln x-x\) equals \(-1\) at \(1\), and \(x\ln x\to 0\) as \(x\to 0^+\), so the value is \(-1\).

## Final answer

\[
-1.
