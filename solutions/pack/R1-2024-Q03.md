## Problem

\[
\int\frac{\ln x+1}{x^{x}+x^{-x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The solutions PDF uses \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^{x})=(\ln x+1)x^{x}\). Writing \(u=x^{x}\),
\[
\frac{\ln x+1}{x^{x}+x^{-x}}=\frac{(\ln x+1)x^{x}}{(x^{x})^{2}+1}=\frac{u'}{u^{2}+1}.
\]
The antiderivative is \(\arctan(x^{x})+C\). The mark scheme prints \(\arctan(x^{x})+C\).

## Final answer

\[
\arctan(x^{x})+C.
\]
