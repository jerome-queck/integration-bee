## Problem

\[
\int\sqrt{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[4]{x\cdots}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The nested radical is \(\bigl(x\cdot(x\cdot(x\cdots)^{1/4})^{1/3}\bigr)^{1/2}\). The exponent of \(x\) is
\[
\sum_{m\ge 2}\frac{1}{m!}=e-2,
\]
because the factor sitting under the first \(m-1\) roots contributes \(\prod_{k=2}^{m}1/k=1/m!\). The integrand is \(x^{e-2}\), and an antiderivative is
\[
\frac{x^{e-1}}{e-1}.
\]
The mark scheme prints that expression. An arbitrary constant may be added.

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{x^{e-1}}{e-1}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy sums \(\sum_{k\ge 2}1/k!=e-2\), and differentiates \(x^{e-1}/(e-1)\) back to \(x^{e-2}\) for \(x>0\).
