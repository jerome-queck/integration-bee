## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}4^{-\lfloor x\rfloor}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On each interval \([n,n+1)\) the floor is \(n\), so the worked PDF splits the integral as a geometric series:
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}4^{-\lfloor x\rfloor}\,dx=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\int_{n}^{n+1}4^{-n}\,dx=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}4^{-n}=\frac{1}{1-1/4}=\frac{4}{3}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(4/3\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{4}{3}.
\]
