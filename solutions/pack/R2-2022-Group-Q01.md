## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\Bigl(\frac1x-\cot x\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

An antiderivative is \(\ln(x/\sin x)=\ln x-\ln\sin x\). As \(x\downarrow 0\), \(\sin x\sim x\), so the logarithm tends to \(0\). At \(x=1\) it equals \(-\ln(\sin 1)\). The integral is \(-\ln(\sin 1)\).

## Final answer

\[
-\ln(\sin 1).
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy simplifies the derivative of \(\ln(x/\sin x)\) minus \(1/x-\cot x\) to \(0\) for \(x>0\), and the limit of the antiderivative at \(0^{+}\) is \(0\).
