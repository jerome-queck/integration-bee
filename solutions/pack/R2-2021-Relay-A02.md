## Problem

The number received from shuttle A1 is \(a=4\). Evaluate
\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+\pi/a)}\,dx.
\]
The value has the form \(\pi/b\). Pass on \(b\).

## Solution

With \(a=4\), the shift is \(\pi/4\). Then \(\sin(x+\pi/4)=(\sin x+\cos x)/\sqrt{2}\), and
\[
\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+\pi/4)}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Biggl(1+\frac{\sin x-\cos x}{\sin x+\cos x}\Biggr).
\]
The second fraction equals \(\tan(x-\pi/4)\), which is odd about \(x=\pi/4\). Its integral on \([0,\pi/2]\) vanishes, and
\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+\pi/4)}\,dx=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{2}}.
\]
Thus \(b=2\sqrt{2}\). The mark scheme prints \(2\sqrt{2}\).

## Final answer

\[
2\sqrt{2}.
\]
