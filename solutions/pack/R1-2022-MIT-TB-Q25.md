## Problem

\[
\int_0^{169}\frac{\pi\sin(\pi\sqrt{x})}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=\sqrt{x}\), so \(dx=2u\,du\) and the upper limit \(169\) becomes \(13\). The powers of \(u\) cancel:
\[
\int_0^{13}2\pi\sin(\pi u)\,du=-2\cos(\pi u)\Big|_0^{13}=-2\bigl(\cos(13\pi)-1\bigr)=4.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(4\).

4.
