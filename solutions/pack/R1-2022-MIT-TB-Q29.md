## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\infty}\frac{1}{\bigl(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\bigr)^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Clear the inner denominator:
\[
\Bigl(x+\frac1x\Bigr)^{2}=\frac{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}{x^{2}},
\]
so the integrand is \(x^{2}/(x^{2}+1)^{2}\). SymPy integrates that rational function from \(0\) to \(\infty\). The same value follows from writing \(x^{2}=(x^{2}+1)-1\) and evaluating \(\int_0^{\infty}(1/(x^{2}+1)-1/(x^{2}+1)^{2})\,dx=\pi/2-\pi/4\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{4}.
\]
