## Problem

\[
\int_{\pi}^{3\pi}\max\bigl(\min(x,\sin x),\min(x,\cos x)\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \([\pi,3\pi]\) one has \(x\ge\pi>1\), so \(\min(x,\sin x)=\sin x\) and \(\min(x,\cos x)=\cos x\). The integrand is \(\max(\sin x,\cos x)\). That function has period \(2\pi\), and the interval of integration has length \(2\pi\), so the integral equals the integral over any other period. On \([\pi/4,5\pi/4]\) the sine is the larger of the two, and on \([5\pi/4,9\pi/4]\) the cosine is:
\[
\int_{\pi/4}^{5\pi/4}\sin x\,dx+\int_{5\pi/4}^{9\pi/4}\cos x\,dx=\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{2}=2\sqrt{2}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
2\sqrt{2}.
\]
