## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\sin(\arccos x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \([0,1]\), \(\sin(\arccos x)=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\). The integral is the area of a quarter disk of radius \(1\),
\[
\int_0^1\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{4}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{4}.
\]
