## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos(\ln x)}{(1+x)^2}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF takes the real part of a Beta integral. For \(x>0\),

\[
x^{i}=\cos(\ln x)+i\sin(\ln x),
\]

and

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^{i}}{(1+x)^2}\,dx=B(1+i,1-i)=\frac{\Gamma(1+i)\Gamma(1-i)}{\Gamma(2)}.
\]

The reflection formula \(\Gamma(z)\Gamma(1-z)=\pi/\sin(\pi z)\) with \(\Gamma(1+i)=i\Gamma(i)\) reduces the product to \(\pi/\sinh\pi\). SymPy returns that value directly, and the value is real, so the cosine integral equals it and the sine integral vanishes.

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{\sinh\pi}.
\]
