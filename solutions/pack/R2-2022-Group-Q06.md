## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{\bigl(x+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\bigr)^{2}}.
\]

## Solution

Set \(x=\sinh u\), so \(u\) runs from \(0\) to \(\infty\) and \(dx=\cosh u\,du\). The identity \(x+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}=e^{u}\) turns the integrand into
\[
\cosh u\,e^{-2u}=\frac{e^{-u}+e^{-3u}}{2}.
\]
Integrating term by term,
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-u}+e^{-3u}}{2}\,du=\frac12+\frac16=\frac23.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{2}{3}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy rewrites \(\cosh u\,e^{-2u}-(e^{-u}+e^{-3u})/2\) in exponentials and expands the difference to \(0\). It evaluates the integral of \((e^{-u}+e^{-3u})/2\) from \(0\) to \(\infty\) as \(2/3\).
