## Problem

\[
\int\frac{(x^{2}+x+1)(x^{3}-x+1)}{x^{5}+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Expanding the numerator gives \(x^{5}+x^{4}+1\), so the integrand is
\[
\frac{x^{5}+x^{4}+1}{x^{5}+1}=1+\frac{x^{4}}{x^{5}+1}.
\]
The antiderivative is \(x+\tfrac15\ln(x^{5}+1)+C\), for \(x^{5}+1>0\). The mark scheme prints that expression.

## Final answer

\[
x+\dfrac{1}{5}\ln(x^{5}+1)+C.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy expands \((x^{2}+x+1)(x^{3}-x+1)-(x^{5}+x^{4}+1)\) to \(0\), and differentiates \(x+\ln(x^{5}+1)/5\) back to \((x^{5}+x^{4}+1)/(x^{5}+1)\).
