## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\operatorname{Si}(x)\sin x}{x}\,dx,
\qquad
\operatorname{Si}(x)=\int_0^x\frac{\sin t}{t}\,dt.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF recognises a derivative of a square:

\[
\frac{d}{dx}\operatorname{Si}(x)^2=2\operatorname{Si}(x)\frac{\sin x}{x}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\operatorname{Si}(x)\sin x}{x}\,dx=\frac12\operatorname{Si}(\infty)^2=\frac12\Biggl(\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin t}{t}\,dt\Biggr)^2=\frac12\Bigl(\frac\pi2\Bigr)^2=\frac{\pi^2}{8}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi^2}{8}.
\]
