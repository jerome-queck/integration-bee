## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-px^{2}}-e^{-qx^{2}}}{x^{2}}\,dx,\qquad p,q>0.
\]

## Solution

Differentiate the exponential in a parameter:
\[
\frac{e^{-px^{2}}-e^{-qx^{2}}}{x^{2}}=\int_{p}^{q}e^{-tx^{2}}\,dt.
\]
The solutions PDF writes the \(t\)-limits in the opposite order together with a minus sign, then swaps that order before the Gaussian step. Either way,
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-px^{2}}-e^{-qx^{2}}}{x^{2}}\,dx=\int_{p}^{q}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-tx^{2}}\,dx\,dt=\int_{p}^{q}\frac12\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{t}}\,dt=\sqrt{\pi}(\sqrt{q}-\sqrt{p}).
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\sqrt{\pi}(\sqrt{q}-\sqrt{p})\).

## Final answer

\[
\sqrt{\pi}(\sqrt{q}-\sqrt{p}).
\]
