## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{e^{-x}}{\sqrt{x}\cdot\sqrt[x]{e}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The \(x\)-th root of \(e\) is \(e^{1/x}\). Set \(u=\sqrt{x}\). The integral becomes
\[
2\int_0^\infty\exp\bigl(-u^2-u^{-2}\bigr)\,du=\frac1{e^2}\int_{-\infty}^\infty\exp\bigl(-(u-u^{-1})^2\bigr)\,du,
\]
after extending the even integrand. The solutions PDF records Glasser's master theorem as one route to a Gaussian. The elementary route in the same note uses the reciprocal substitution \(u\mapsto 1/u\), which supplies the missing factor \(1/u^2\). Adding the two copies produces
\[
\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-v^2-2}\,dv=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{e^2},
\]
with \(v=u-1/u\). The mark scheme prints \(\sqrt{\pi}/e^2\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\sqrt\pi}{e^2}.
\]
