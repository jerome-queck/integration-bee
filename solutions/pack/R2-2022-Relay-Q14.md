## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1/2}\Bigl\lfloor\frac{1}{x}\Bigr\rfloor\Bigl\lceil\frac{1}{x}\Bigr\rceil\ln\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{\lfloor 1/x\rfloor^{2}}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On each interval \((1/(n+1),1/n]\) with \(n\ge 2\), one has \(\lfloor 1/x\rfloor=n\) and \(\lceil 1/x\rceil=n+1\). The length is \(1/(n(n+1))\), so the floors cancel and the piece is
\[
\ln\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{n^{2}}\Bigr)=\ln\frac{(n-1)(n+1)}{n^{2}}.
\]
The partial product from \(n=2\) to \(N\) is \((N+1)/(2N)\), whose logarithm tends to \(-\ln 2\). The mark scheme prints \(-\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
-\ln 2.
\]
