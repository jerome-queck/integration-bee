## Problem

\[
\lim_{A\to\infty}\frac{1}{A}\int_{1}^{A}A^{1/x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The marked value \(1\) selects the integrand \(A^{1/x}\). Since \(A^{1/x}\ge 1\), the liminf is at least \(1\). Write \(L=\ln A\). On \([1,2]\),
\[
\frac{A^{1/x}}{A}=\exp\Bigl(-L\frac{x-1}{x}\Bigr)\le e^{-Lu/2}
\]
after \(u=x-1\), and \(\int_{0}^{1}e^{-Lu/2}\,du\le 2/L\to 0\). On \([2,A^{1/4}]\) the integral is at most \(A^{-1/4}\to 0\). On \([A^{1/4},A]\), \(A^{1/x}\) tends uniformly to \(1\), and \((A-A^{1/4})/A\to 1\). The limit is \(1\). The mark scheme prints \(1\).
