## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{4}+2ax^{2}+1},\qquad a>1.
\]

## Solution

Factor the denominator as \((x^{2}+p)(x^{2}+q)\) with
\[
p=a+\sqrt{a^{2}-1},\qquad q=a-\sqrt{a^{2}-1}.
\]
Then \(pq=1\), \(p+q=2a\), and \(p>q>0\). Partial fractions give
\[
\frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\frac{1/\sqrt{q}-1/\sqrt{p}}{p-q}.
\]
Denesting this expression produces
\[
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{2(a+1)}}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/(2\sqrt{2(a+1)})\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{2(a+1)}}.
\]
