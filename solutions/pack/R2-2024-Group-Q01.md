## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{dx}{1+(x+x^{2}+x^{3}+\cdots)^{2024}}.
\]

## Solution

For \(x\in[0,1)\) the geometric sum is \(x/(1-x)\), so the integrand is
\[
\frac{(1-x)^{2024}}{(1-x)^{2024}+x^{2024}}.
\]
The reflection \(x\mapsto 1-x\) swaps the two powers in the denominator. The integrand and its reflection therefore add to \(1\), and
\[
2I=\int_{0}^{1}1\,dx=1.
\]
The same cancellation holds with \(2024\) replaced by any positive integer. The mark scheme prints \(1/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{2}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

For a positive integer \(n\) and \(t\in(0,1)\), SymPy simplifies
\[
\frac{(1-t)^{n}}{(1-t)^{n}+t^{n}}+\frac{t^{n}}{(1-t)^{n}+t^{n}}
\]
to \(1\). Half of \(\int_{0}^{1}1\,dx\) is \(1/2\).
