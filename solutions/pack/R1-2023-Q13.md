## Problem

\[
\int_{-1}^{0}5(x^{6}+x)^{4}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF factors the integrand as a derivative:
\[
5(x^{6}+x)^{4}=5x^{4}(x^{5}+1)^{4}=\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{(x^{5}+1)^{5}}{5}\right).
\]
Evaluating from \(-1\) to \(0\) gives
\[
\frac{(0+1)^{5}}{5}-\frac{((-1)^{5}+1)^{5}}{5}=\frac{1}{5}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(1/5\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{5}.
\]
