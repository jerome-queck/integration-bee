## Problem

\[
\int\frac{x^{n}}{1+x+\dfrac{x^{2}}{2!}+\cdots+\dfrac{x^{n}}{n!}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The problem sheet adds the aside ``maybe replace this with Finn's question.'' The integral above is the one printed there. Let \(n\) be a nonnegative integer and
\[
u=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}.
\]
Then \(u'=u-x^{n}/n!\), so
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\bigl(n!\,x-n!\ln u\bigr)=n!-n!\frac{u'}{u}=\frac{x^{n}}{u}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(n!\,x-n!\log(1+x+\cdots+x^{n}/n!)\), with \(\log\) the natural logarithm.

## Final answer

\[
n!\,x-n!\ln\Bigl(1+x+\cdots+\dfrac{x^{n}}{n!}\Bigr).
\]
