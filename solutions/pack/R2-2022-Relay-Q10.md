## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-ax}\operatorname{Ei}(x)\,dx,\qquad \operatorname{Ei}(x)=\int_{x}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-t}}{t}\,dt,
\]

## Solution

The certificate takes \(a>0\), the positive-parameter convention used in this derivation. The printed problem does not state a parameter domain. The paper's \(\operatorname{Ei}\) is the exponential integral \(E_{1}\). Switch the order:
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-ax}E_{1}(x)\,dx=\frac{1}{a}\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-t}-e^{-(a+1)t}}{t}\,dt.
\]
Differentiating the Frullani integral in \(a\) produces \(\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-(a+1)t}\,dt=1/(a+1)\), which is also the derivative of \(\ln(1+a)\). Both sides vanish at \(a=0\), so the Frullani integral equals \(\ln(1+a)\). The original integral is \(\ln(1+a)/a\). The mark scheme prints \(\log(1+a)/a\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\log(1+a)}{a}.
\]
