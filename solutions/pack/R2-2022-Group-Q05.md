## Problem

\[
\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{\lfloor x\rfloor}}{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \([n,n+1)\) the integrand is \((-1)^{n}/x\), so the integral is
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\ln\frac{n+1}{n}.
\]
The partial sum through \(2N\) is
\[
\ln\prod_{k=1}^{N}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{4k^{2}}\Biggr).
\]
The infinite product for the sine at \(z=1/2\) is
\[
\prod_{k=1}^{\infty}\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{4k^{2}}\Biggr)=\frac{\sin(\pi/2)}{\pi/2}=\frac{2}{\pi}.
\]
The partial product equals \(2\,\Gamma(N+\tfrac12)\Gamma(N+\tfrac32)/(\pi\,\Gamma(N+1)^{2})\), whose limit is \(2/\pi\). The integral is \(\ln(2/\pi)\). The mark scheme prints \(\ln(2/\pi)\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln\frac{2}{\pi}.
\]
