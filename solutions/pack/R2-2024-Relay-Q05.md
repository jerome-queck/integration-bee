## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{1+\bigl(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\bigr)^{2}}.
\]

## Solution

Clearing the inner denominator gives the rational function
\[
\frac{x^{2}}{x^{4}+3x^{2}+1},
\]
understood at \(x=0\) by continuity (the original integrand is even in the principal-value sense and the rational function is integrable). The same algebra is
\[
\frac{1}{5+(x-1/x)^{2}}.
\]
Glasser's master theorem replaces \(x-1/x\) by \(x\) for this decaying integrand, and
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{5+x^{2}}=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{5}}.
\]
Factoring \(x^{4}+3x^{2}+1\) and integrating the partial fractions produces the same value. The mark scheme prints \(\pi/\sqrt{5}\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{\sqrt{5}}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy simplifies \(1+(x+1/x)^{2}-(x^{4}+3x^{2}+1)/x^{2}\) to \(0\) and evaluates
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^{2}}{x^{4}+3x^{2}+1}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{5}}.
\]
