## Problem

\[
\int_{420}^{1672}\frac{\sqrt{\ln(2023-x)}}{\sqrt{\ln(2023-x)}+\sqrt{\ln(x-69)}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The problem sheet uses \(\ln(x-69)\). The worked PDF writes \(\log(69+x)\) in that place. The two integrands are different, and the king substitution on the printed limits swaps \(\ln(2023-x)\) with \(\ln(x-69)\), not with \(\ln(69+x)\). The problem-sheet integrand is the one evaluated here.

Let \(f(x)\) be that integrand. The endpoints sum to \(2092\), and
\[
2023-(2092-x)=x-69,\qquad (2092-x)-69=2023-x,
\]
so \(f(x)+f(2092-x)=1\). Therefore
\[
2I=\int_{420}^{1672}1\,dx=1252,\qquad I=626.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(626\).

## Final answer

\[
626.
\]
