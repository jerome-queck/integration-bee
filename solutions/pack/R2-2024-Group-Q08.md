## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\operatorname{argmax}_{y\in\mathbb{R}}
\Biggl(
x^{\sqrt{1-x^{4}}\,y^{2}+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\,y-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\,y-\sqrt{1-x^{8}}}
\Biggr)\,dx,
\]
where \(\operatorname{argmax}_{y\in A}f(y)\) is the value of \(y\in A\) that maximises \(f\).

## Solution

The solutions PDF observes that for fixed \(x\in(0,1)\) the power \(x^{t}\) decreases as \(t\) increases, so the displayed expression is maximised when the exponent is minimised. The exponent is a convex quadratic in \(y\), and its vertex is
\[
y=\frac{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}-\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}{2\sqrt{1-x^{4}}}
=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}-\frac{1}{2\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}.
\]
The integral of that vertex is
\[
\frac12\Bigl[\sinh^{-1}x-\sin^{-1}x\Bigr]_{0}^{1}
=\frac{\sinh^{-1}1}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}
=\frac{\ln(1+\sqrt{2})}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}.
\]
The mark scheme prints both forms.

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\ln(1+\sqrt{2})}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}.
\]
