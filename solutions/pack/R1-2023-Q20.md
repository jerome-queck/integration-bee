## Problem

\[
\int_{1/4}^{3/4}f(f(x))\,dx,\qquad f(x)=\frac{4^x}{2+4^x}.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF uses \(f(1-x)=1-f(x)\). King's substitution on an interval symmetric about \(1/2\) then gives

\[
I=\int_{1/4}^{3/4}f(f(1-x))\,dx=\int_{1/4}^{3/4}f(1-f(x))\,dx=\int_{1/4}^{3/4}\bigl(1-f(f(x))\bigr)\,dx.
\]

Adding the two expressions for \(I\),

\[
2I=\int_{1/4}^{3/4}1\,dx=\frac12,\qquad I=\frac14.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac14.
\]
