## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\arctan(e^{x})}{\cosh x}\cdot\frac{\tanh(x/2)}{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Write \(g\) for the integrand and \(k(x)=\tanh(x/2)/\cosh x\). The sum \(\arctan(e^{x})+\arctan(e^{-x})\) is constantly \(\pi/2\), and
\[
g(x)+g(-x)=\frac{\pi}{2}\frac{k(x)}{x}.
\]
The quotient \(k(x)/x\) is even, so the integral of \(g\) is \((\pi/2)\int_{0}^{\infty}k(x)/x\,dx\). The identity \(k(x)=\tanh x-\tanh(x/2)\) rewrites \(k\) as
\[
k(x)=\int_{1/2}^{1}x\operatorname{sech}^{2}(ax)\,da.
\]
Thus \(\int_{0}^{\infty}k(x)/x\,dx=\int_{1/2}^{1}da/a=\ln 2\), and the original integral is \((\pi/2)\ln 2\). The mark scheme prints \((\pi/2)\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{2}\ln 2.
\]
