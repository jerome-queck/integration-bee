## Problem

\[
\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{\sin\sqrt{x}}{\sinh\sqrt{x}+\sin\sqrt{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF takes the square root of a negative number through \(\sqrt{-t}=i\sqrt{t}\) and the identity \(\sinh z=-i\sin(iz)\). The substitution \(x=-u\) produces

\[
I=\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{\sinh\sqrt{u}}{\sinh\sqrt{u}+\sin\sqrt{u}}\,du.
\]

Adding the two copies of \(I\) cancels the denominator:

\[
2I=\int_{-1}^{1}1\,dx=2,\qquad I=1.
\]

## Final answer

\[
1.
\]
