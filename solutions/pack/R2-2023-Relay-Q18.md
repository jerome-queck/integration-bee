## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{1+\tan^{2024}x}.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=1/(1+\tan^{2024}x)\). Then \(\tan(\pi/2-x)=\cot x\), and
\[
f(\pi/2-x)=\frac{1}{1+\cot^{2024}x}=\frac{\tan^{2024}x}{1+\tan^{2024}x}.
\]
Adding the two copies,
\[
f(x)+f(\pi/2-x)=1
\]
at every \(x\) where the tangent is defined and positive; the endpoints do not affect the integral. Therefore \(2I=\pi/2\), and \(I=\pi/4\). The same argument works for every positive power in place of \(2024\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{4}.
\]
