## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{1}{1+e^{ax}}\,dx,\qquad a>0.
\]

## Solution

For \(x>0\),
\[
\frac{1}{1+e^{ax}}=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}e^{-anx}.
\]
Integrating term by term,
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}\int_0^\infty e^{-anx}\,dx=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{an}=\frac{\ln 2}{a}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\ln 2/a\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\ln 2}{a}.
\]
