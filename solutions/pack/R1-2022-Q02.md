## Problem

\[
\int_0^{100}\lceil x\rceil\lfloor x\rfloor\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On each open interval \((n-1,n)\), with \(n=1,2,\ldots,100\), one has \(\lfloor x\rfloor=n-1\) and \(\lceil x\rceil=n\). Values at the integers do not affect the integral. The worked PDF therefore writes

\[
\sum_{n=1}^{100}\int_{n-1}^{n} n(n-1)\,dx=\sum_{n=1}^{100}n(n-1)=\sum_{n=1}^{100}(n^2-n).
\]

The closed forms \(\sum n^2=N(N+1)(2N+1)/6\) and \(\sum n=N(N+1)/2\) at \(N=100\) give

\[
\frac{100\cdot 101\cdot 201}{6}-\frac{100\cdot 101}{2}=338350-5050=333300.
\]

## Final answer

\[
333300.
\]
