## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\frac{\sin(\log x)-\log x}{(\log x)^2}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=-\log x\). The integral becomes
\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{u-\sin u}{u^2}e^{-u}\,du.
\]
Insert \(1/u^2=\int_0^\infty t e^{-tu}\,dt\) and integrate in \(u\) first:
\[
\int_0^\infty t\Bigl(\frac{1}{(1+t)^2}-\frac{1}{(1+t)^2+1}\Bigr)\,dt.
\]
The antiderivative is \(\ln(1+t)+1/(1+t)-\frac12\ln((1+t)^2+1)+\arctan(1+t)\). Its limit from \(0\) to \(\infty\) is \(\ln 2/2+\pi/4-1\). The mark scheme prints \(\ln 2/2+\pi/4-1\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\ln 2}{2}+\frac\pi4-1.
\]
