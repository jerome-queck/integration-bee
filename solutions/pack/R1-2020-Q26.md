## Problem

\[
\int\cos(\ln x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF takes the real part of \(\int x^{i}\,dx\):
\[
\int x^{i}\,dx=\frac{x^{1+i}}{1+i}+C.
\]
Since \(1/(1+i)=(1-i)/2\) and \(x^{i}=\cos(\ln x)+i\sin(\ln x)\),
\[
\operatorname{Re}\frac{x^{1+i}}{1+i}=\frac{x}{2}\bigl(\cos(\ln x)+\sin(\ln x)\bigr).
\]
The answers sheet prints \(\frac{x}{2}\sin(\ln x)+\frac{x}{2}\cos(\ln x)+C\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{x}{2}\cos(\ln x)+\dfrac{x}{2}\sin(\ln x)+C.
\]
