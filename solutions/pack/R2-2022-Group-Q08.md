## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{2\pi}\frac{\sin(24x)}{\sin x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The Dirichlet quotient expands as a sum of cosines of odd multiples of \(x\):
\[
\frac{\sin(24x)}{\sin x}=2\sum_{j=0}^{11}\cos((2j+1)x).
\]
Each integer frequency \(k\ge 1\) satisfies \(\int_{0}^{2\pi}\cos(kx)\,dx=0\). The integral is \(0\). At \(x=0\) the integrand extends by continuity to the value \(24\), which does not affect the integral.
