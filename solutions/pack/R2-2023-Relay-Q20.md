## Problem

If
\[
\bigl(f(x)\bigr)^{2}=\int_{0}^{x}\frac{t f(t)}{1+t^{2}}\,dt,
\]
compute \(\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)\,dx\). The original page supplies no positivity or nonzero condition on \(f\).

## Source conflict

The function \(f_0(x)=0\) satisfies the equation everywhere and gives integral \(0\). The smooth function
\[
f_1(x)=\frac14\log(1+x^2)
\]
also satisfies it: both sides vanish at \(x=0\), and
\[
\frac{d}{dx}f_1(x)^2
=\frac{x\log(1+x^2)}{4(1+x^2)}
=\frac{x f_1(x)}{1+x^2}.
\]
Its integral is
\[
\int_{-1}^{1}f_1(x)\,dx
=\frac{\pi+\log 4-4}{4}>0,
\]
which agrees with the printed mark. The two different values arise from two valid solutions of the same printed equation.

Differentiation yields \(2ff'=xf/(1+x^2)\). Cancelling \(f\) requires a nonvanishing hypothesis that the problem does not state. Merely selecting the solution that reproduces the mark would not prove the original problem. The row therefore stays manual; no transcript replacing the unknown \(f\) by the selected logarithm is added.
