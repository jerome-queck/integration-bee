## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\sqrt{2^x\sqrt{2^{2x}\sqrt{2^{3x}\sqrt{2^{4x}\cdots}}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Expand the nested radicals by exponents:

\[
\sqrt{2^x\sqrt{2^{2x}\sqrt{2^{3x}\cdots}}}=2^{\sum_{n=1}^\infty n x/2^n}.
\]

The series \(\sum_{n\ge 1} n r^n=r/(1-r)^2\) at \(r=1/2\) equals \(2\), so the exponent is \(2x\) and the integrand is \(2^{2x}=4^x\). Then

\[
\int_0^1 4^x\,dx=\frac{4-1}{\ln 4}=\frac{3}{2\ln 2}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{3}{2\ln 2}.
\]
