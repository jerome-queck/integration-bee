## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\ln x\sin x}{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF inserts Frullani's representation \(\ln x=\int_0^\infty(e^{-t}-e^{-xt})/t\,dt\) and changes the order of integration. The inner Dirichlet integral with an exponential factor is

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x}{x}e^{-sx}\,dx=\arctan\frac1s=\frac\pi2-\arctan s
\]

for \(s>0\), and \(\int_0^\infty(\sin x)/x\,dx=\pi/2\). The \(t\)-integral that remains is

\[
\frac\pi2\int_0^\infty\ln(t)\,e^{-t}\,dt=-\frac{\gamma\pi}{2},
\]

after the boundary term in the worked PDF's integration by parts vanishes. The mark scheme prints \(-\gamma\pi/2\).

## Final answer

\[
-\frac{\gamma\pi}{2}.
\]
