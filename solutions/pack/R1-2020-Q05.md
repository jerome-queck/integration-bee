## Problem

\[
\int\sqrt{\tan x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF sets \(u=\sqrt{\tan x}\), so \(\tan x=u^{2}\) and \(\sec^{2}x\,dx=2u\,du\). Since \(\sec^{2}x=1+\tan^{2}x=1+u^{4}\),
\[
\int\sqrt{\tan x}\,dx=\int\frac{2u^{2}}{1+u^{4}}\,du.
\]
The answers sheet prints the antiderivative after returning to the original variable. With the positive square root,
\[
\frac{\sqrt2}{4}\left(
\ln\left|\frac{\tan x-\sqrt{2\tan x}+1}{\tan x+\sqrt{2\tan x}+1}\right|
+2\arctan(\sqrt{2\tan x}-1)
+2\arctan(\sqrt{2\tan x}+1)
\right)+C.
\]
The worked PDF's last displayed line uses different coefficients on the logarithm and the two arctangents. Differentiating the answers-sheet expression recovers \(\sqrt{\tan x}\). The answers sheet wins.

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\left(\ln\left|\dfrac{\tan x-\sqrt{2\tan x}+1}{\tan x+\sqrt{2\tan x}+1}\right|+2\arctan(\sqrt{2\tan x}-1)+2\arctan(\sqrt{2\tan x}+1)\right)+C.
\]
