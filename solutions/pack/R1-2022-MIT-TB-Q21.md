## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\frac{x^{5}-1}{\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(p>0\),
\[
\frac{d}{dp}\int_0^1\frac{x^{p}-1}{\ln x}\,dx=\int_0^1 x^{p}\,dx=\frac{1}{p+1},
\]
and the integral vanishes at \(p=0\). Integrating the derivative from \(0\) to \(5\) gives \(\ln 6\). The mark scheme prints \(\ln 6\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 6.
\]
