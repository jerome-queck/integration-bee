## Problem

\[
\int_{1/\ln 2}^{2024}\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{2^{kx/n}\,k x^4}{nkx^3+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Rewrite each summand as
\[
2^{kx/n}\frac{x}{n+1/(kx^3)}=\frac{x}{n}\,2^{(k/n)x}\frac{1}{1+1/(nkx^3)}.
\]
For \(x>0\) the fraction in the denominator lies between \(1\) and \(1+1/(nx^3)\). The resulting upper and lower sums are Riemann sums for \(\int_0^x 2^u\,du\), and the squeeze in the solutions PDF passes to the limit
\[
\int_0^x 2^u\,du=\frac{2^x-1}{\ln 2}.
\]
The outer antiderivative is \((2^x/\ln 2-x)/\ln 2\). From \(1/\ln 2\) to \(2024\), and using \(2^{1/\ln 2}=e\),
\[
\frac1{\ln 2}\left(\frac{2^{2024}-e+1}{\ln 2}-2024\right).
\]
The mark scheme prints that value.

## Final answer

\[
\frac1{\ln 2}\left(\frac{2^{2024}-e+1}{\ln 2}-2024\right).
\]
