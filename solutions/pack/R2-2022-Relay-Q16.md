## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-x}\sqrt{1-e^{-2x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=e^{-x}\). Then \(du=-e^{-x}\,dx\), and \(u\) runs from \(1\) to \(0\), so
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{1-u^{2}}\,du.
\]
This is the area of a quarter disk of radius \(1\), equal to \(\pi/4\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{4}.
\]
