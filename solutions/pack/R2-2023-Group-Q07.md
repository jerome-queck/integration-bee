## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\max_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\Bigl(\frac{\lfloor 2^{n+1}x\rfloor}{2^{n}}-\frac{\lfloor 2^{n}x\rfloor}{2^{n-1}}\Bigr)\,dx,
\]

## Solution

with \(0\in\mathbb{N}\). If \(\lfloor 2^{n}x\rfloor=m\) and the next binary digit is \(b\in\{0,1\}\), the expression inside the maximum is \(b/2^{n}\). On the dyadic interval \([2^{-k},2^{-k+1})\) the leading digit sits at place \(k\), so the maximum is the constant \(2^{1-k}\). The integral is
\[
\sum_{k\ge 1}2^{1-k}\cdot 2^{-k}=\sum_{k\ge 1}2^{1-2k}=\frac{2}{3}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(2/3\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{2}{3}.
\]
