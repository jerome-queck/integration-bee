## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{x\tan x-1}{\sqrt{\sec^{2}x-x^{2}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(F(x)=\arcsin(x\cos x)\). Then
\[
F'(x)=\frac{\cos x-x\sin x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}\cos^{2}x}}.
\]
On \((0,\pi/2)\),
\[
\sqrt{1-x^{2}\cos^{2}x}=\cos x\sqrt{\sec^{2}x-x^{2}}
\]
and \(\cos x-x\sin x=\cos x\,(1-x\tan x)\), so \(F'\) is the negative of the printed integrand. The endpoints are \(F(0)=0\) and \(F(\pi/2)=0\). The integral is \(0\). The mark scheme prints \(0\).
