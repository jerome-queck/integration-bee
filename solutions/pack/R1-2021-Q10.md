## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{x}{\sin x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The solutions PDF writes \(1/\sin x=2i e^{-ix}/(1-e^{-2ix})\) and expands the geometric series. Integration by parts produces
\[
\pi i\sum_{n\ge 0}\frac{(-1)^{n}}{2n+1}+2\sum_{n\ge 0}\frac{(-1)^{n}}{(2n+1)^{2}}-2i\sum_{n\ge 0}\frac{1}{(2n+1)^{2}}.
\]
Those series are \(\pi/4\), Catalan's constant \(G\), and \(\pi^{2}/8\). The imaginary parts \(\pi i\cdot\pi/4-2i\cdot\pi^{2}/8\) cancel, leaving \(2G\). The mark scheme prints \(2G\).

## Final answer

\[
2G.
\]
