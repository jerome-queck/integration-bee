## Problem

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\bigl((n+1)(n+2)\cdots(2n)\bigr)^{1/n}}{n}.
\]

## Solution

The product is \((2n)!/n!\). Stirling's approximation \(\ln(k!)=k\ln k-k+O(\ln k)\) gives
\begin{align*}
\frac1n\bigl(\ln((2n)!)-\ln(n!)\bigr)-\ln n
&=2\ln(2n)-\ln n-1-\ln n+o(1)\\
&=2\ln 2-1+o(1).
\end{align*}
Exponentiating, the limit is \(4/e\). The same limit is the gamma form
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\Gamma(2n+1)^{1/n}}{n\,\Gamma(n+1)^{1/n}}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{4}{e}.
\]

SymPy evaluates \(\lim_{n\to\infty}\Gamma(2n+1)^{1/n}/(n\,\Gamma(n+1)^{1/n})=4e^{-1}\).
