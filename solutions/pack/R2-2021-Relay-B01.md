## Problem

Evaluate
\[
\int_{0}^{2\pi}\sin(\sin x-x)\,dx.
\]
Pass on the value.

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=\sin(\sin x-x)\). Then
\[
f(2\pi-x)=\sin\bigl(\sin(2\pi-x)-(2\pi-x)\bigr)=\sin(-\sin x+x-2\pi)=\sin(x-\sin x)=-f(x),
\]
using \(\sin(2\pi-x)=\sin x\) and the \(2\pi\)-period of sine. The integrand is odd about \(x=\pi\), so the integral on \([0,2\pi]\) is \(0\). The mark scheme prints \(0\).
