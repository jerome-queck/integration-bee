## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\frac{\ln(1-x)}{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \([0,1)\),
\[
\frac{\ln(1-x)}{x}=-\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{x^{n-1}}{n}.
\]
Integrating term by term produces \(-\sum_{n\ge 1}1/n^{2}=-\pi^{2}/6\). The mark scheme prints \(-\pi^{2}/6\).

## Final answer

\[
-\dfrac{\pi^{2}}{6}.
\]
