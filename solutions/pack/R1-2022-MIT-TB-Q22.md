## Problem

\[
\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^{2022}x\,dx.
\]

## Solution

\(\cos^{2022}(x+\pi/2)=\sin^{2022}x\), so the integrand is not periodic of period \(\pi/2\). On \([0,\pi/2]\) the integrals of \(\cos^{2022}\) and \(\sin^{2022}\) agree, and the four quadrants are equal by that identity and by shifting. Therefore
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^{2022}x\,dx=4\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^{2022}x\,dx.
\]
Wallis' formula gives
\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^{2022}x\,dx=\frac{\pi}{2}\cdot\frac{\binom{2022}{1011}}{2^{2022}},
\]
so the full integral is \(\pi\binom{2022}{1011}/2^{2021}\). The mark scheme prints that value.

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{2^{2021}}\binom{2022}{1011}.
\]
