## Problem

\[
\int_0^{1}\frac{\arctan x+\operatorname{arccot} x}{x^{2}+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(x>0\), \(\arctan x+\operatorname{arccot} x=\pi/2\). The integrand is therefore the constant \(\pi/2\) times \(1/(x^{2}+1)\), and
\[
\int_0^{1}\frac{\pi/2}{x^{2}+1}\,dx=\frac\pi2\cdot\frac\pi4=\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi^{2}/8\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi^{2}}{8}.
\]
