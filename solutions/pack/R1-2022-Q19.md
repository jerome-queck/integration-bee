## Problem

\[
\int_0^\pi\sec x\ln\Bigl(1+\frac{\cos x}{3}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(|a|<1\) set \(I(a)=\int_0^\pi\sec x\ln(1+a\cos x)\,dx\), so the printed integral is \(I(1/3)\). Differentiating under the integral and using \(t=\tan(x/2)\) gives
\[
I'(a)=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}.
\]
Then \(I(0)=0\), and
\[
I\Bigl(\frac13\Bigr)=\int_0^{1/3}\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{1-s^2}}\,ds=\pi\arcsin\frac13.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi\arcsin(1/3)\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi\arcsin\frac13.
\]
