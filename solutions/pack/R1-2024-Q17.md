## Problem

\[
\int_0^{1+\pi/4}\tan\bigl(x-\tan(x-\tan(x-\cdots))\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(y\) be the unique solution of \(y=\tan(x-y)\) that stays in \([0,1]\) as \(x\) runs from \(0\) to \(1+\pi/4\). That is the branch the substitution uses, not an arbitrary infinite iteration. Then \(x=y+\arctan y\) and \(dx=(1+1/(1+y^2))\,dy\). The worked PDF's direct substitution is

\[
\int_0^1 y\Biggl(1+\frac{1}{1+y^2}\Biggr)dy=\frac12+\frac12\ln 2=\frac{1+\ln 2}{2}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{1+\ln 2}{2}.
\]
