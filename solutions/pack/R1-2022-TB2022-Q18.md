## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\ln x\cdot\sin(\ln x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(x=e^{-u}\). Then \(\ln x=-u\) and the integral becomes
\[
\int_0^{\infty}ue^{-u}\sin u\,du=\frac12.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(1/2\).
