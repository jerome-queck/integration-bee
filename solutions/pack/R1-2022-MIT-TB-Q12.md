## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\sqrt{-\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(s=-\ln x\), so \(x=e^{-s}\) and \(dx=e^{-s}\,ds\). The endpoints \(x=1\) and \(x=0\) become \(s=0\) and \(s=\infty\). The integral is the Gamma value
\[
\int_0^\infty s^{1/2}e^{-s}\,ds=\Gamma\Bigl(\frac32\Bigr)=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\sqrt{\pi}/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}.
\]
