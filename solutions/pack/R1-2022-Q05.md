## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\Bigl(\frac1x-\Bigl\lfloor\frac1x\Bigr\rfloor\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

The integrand is the fractional part \(\{1/x\}\).

## Solution

Split the integral at the points \(1/k\), as in the worked PDF:

\[
\int_0^1\Bigl(\frac1x-\Bigl\lfloor\frac1x\Bigr\rfloor\Bigr)\,dx
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^n\int_{1/(k+1)}^{1/k}\Bigl(\frac1x-k\Bigr)dx.
\]

Each piece is \(\ln(k+1)-\ln k-1/(k+1)\). The logarithms telescope, and

\[
\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{1}{k+1}=H_{n+1}-1,
\]

so the partial integral is \(\ln(n+1)-(H_{n+1}-1)\). The limit is \(1-\gamma\).

## Final answer

\[
1-\gamma.
\]
