## Problem

\[
\int\frac{dx}{2+2\sin x+\cos x}.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF uses the Weierstrass substitution \(t=\tan(x/2)\), with

\[
\sin x=\frac{2t}{1+t^2},\qquad\cos x=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2},\qquad dx=\frac{2\,dt}{1+t^2}.
\]

The integrand becomes

\[
\frac{2\,dt}{(t+1)(t+3)}=\Bigl(\frac{1}{t+1}-\frac{1}{t+3}\Bigr)dt.
\]

Integrating and restoring \(t\) gives the answers-sheet expression.

## Final answer

\[
\ln\left|\frac{\tan(x/2)+1}{\tan(x/2)+3}\right|+C.
\]
