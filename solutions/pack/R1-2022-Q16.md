## Problem

\[
\int_1^\infty\frac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x^2}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF says the substitution \(u=1/x\) reduces this integral to problem 5, whose value is \(1-\gamma\). Explicitly, \(x=1/u\) and \(dx=-du/u^2\) give

\[
\int_1^\infty\frac{\{x\}}{x^2}\,dx=\int_0^1\Bigl\{\frac1u\Bigr\}\,du=\int_0^1\Biggl(\frac1u-\Bigl\lfloor\frac1u\Bigr\rfloor\Biggr)du,
\]

which is the integrand of R1-2022-Q05. Splitting at \(1/k\) as in that solution, the partial integral is \(\ln(n+1)-(H_{n+1}-1)\), and the limit is \(1-\gamma\).

## Final answer

\[
1-\gamma.
\]
