## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\cdots}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(y\) denote the nested radical, so \(y=\sqrt{x+y}\) and \(y>0\). Then \(y^2-y-x=0\), and the positive root is

\[
y=\frac12+\sqrt{x+\frac14}.
\]

Integrating,

\[
\int_0^1 y\,dx=\Bigl[\frac23\Bigl(x+\frac14\Bigr)^{3/2}+\frac{x}{2}\Bigr]_0^1=\frac{5}{12}\bigl(1+\sqrt{5}\bigr).
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{5}{12}\bigl(1+\sqrt{5}\bigr).
\]
