## Problem

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\Bigl(\frac{k}{n}\Bigr)^{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The inner sum is a Riemann sum in the variable \(y=k/n\):
\[
\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^{n}\Bigl(\frac{k}{n}\Bigr)^{x}\to\int_{0}^{1}y^{x}\,dy=\frac{1}{x+1}
\]
for each \(x>-1\). The convergence is dominated by \(1\) on \([0,1]\), so the limit passes inside the \(x\)-integral:
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{dx}{x+1}=\ln 2.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\ln 2.
\]

## SymPy self-check

For \(x>0\), SymPy evaluates \(\int_{0}^{1}y^{x}\,dy=1/(x+1)\) and \(\int_{0}^{1}dx/(x+1)=\ln 2\).
