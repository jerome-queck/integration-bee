## Problem

\[
\int
\frac{
8+\dfrac{4+\dfrac{2+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}}{\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x}}}}{\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x}}}}}
{\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x}}}}}
}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(s_{1}=\sqrt{x}\) and \(s_{k+1}=\sqrt{x+s_{k}}\). Differentiating the outermost radical and repeating the same step three more times produces
\[
\frac{d}{dx}s_{4}
=\frac{1}{16}\cdot
\frac{
8+\dfrac{4+\dfrac{2+1/s_{1}}{s_{2}}}{s_{3}}
}{s_{4}}.
\]
Multiplying by \(16\) shows that the integrand is the derivative of \(16 s_{4}\). The mark scheme prints that antiderivative.

## Final answer

\[
16\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x}}}}+C.
\]

## SymPy self-check

With \(s_{1}=\sqrt{x}\) and \(s_{k+1}=\sqrt{x+s_{k}}\), SymPy simplifies the difference between the derivative of \(16 s_{4}\) and the nested fraction to \(0\) for \(x>0\).
