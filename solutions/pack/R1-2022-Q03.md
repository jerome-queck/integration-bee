## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi}\cos(x+\cos x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=\cos(x+\cos x)\). Then
\[
f(\pi-x)=\cos(\pi-x-\cos x)=-\cos(x+\cos x)=-f(x).
\]
The substitution \(u=\pi-x\) therefore sends the integral \(I\) to \(-I\), so \(I=0\). The worked PDF records this reflection. The mark scheme prints \(0\).
