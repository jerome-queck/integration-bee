## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^{a}-x^{b}}{\ln x}\,dx,
\]

## Solution

For \(x>0\),
\[
\int_{b}^{a}x^{s}\,ds=\frac{x^{a}-x^{b}}{\ln x}.
\]
Integrating in \(x\) and using \(\int_{0}^{1}x^{s}\,dx=1/(s+1)\) gives
\[
\int_{2}^{3}\frac{ds}{s+1}=\ln\frac{4}{3}.
\]
Since \(1<4/3<e\), the logarithm lies in \((0,1)\), and the least integer above it is \(1\). The mark scheme prints \(1\).
