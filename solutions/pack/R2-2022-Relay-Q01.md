## Problem

\[
I=\int_{0}^{1}(1-x^{b})^{1/a}\,dx,\qquad J=\int_{0}^{1}(1-x^{a})^{1/b}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Take the root indices to be positive real numbers, \(a,b>0\). This includes positive integral root indices. The paper does not state a domain; the symbolic transcript records these assumptions explicitly, and makes no claim for unrestricted complex indices.

For the first integral set \(x=u^{1/b}\). Then
\[
I=\frac{1}{b}B\Bigl(\frac{1}{b},1+\frac{1}{a}\Bigr)=\frac{1}{ab}\frac{\Gamma(1/a)\Gamma(1/b)}{\Gamma(1+1/a+1/b)},
\]
where \(\Gamma(1+1/a)=\Gamma(1/a)/a\). The expression is symmetric in \(a\) and \(b\), so \(J\) has the same value. The mark scheme prints \(I=J\).

## Final answer

\[
I=J.
\]
