## Problem

The number received from shuttle A2 is \(b=2\sqrt{2}\). Evaluate
\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{1+\tan^{b}x}.
\]
Pass on four times the value.

## Solution

For \(t>0\),
\[
\frac{1}{1+t}+\frac{t}{1+t}=1.
\]
Set \(t=\tan^{b}x\). The substitution \(x\mapsto\pi/2-x\) replaces \(\tan x\) by its reciprocal, so the integrand \(f\) satisfies \(f(x)+f(\pi/2-x)=1\). Therefore
\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}f(x)\,dx=\frac12\int_{0}^{\pi/2}1\,dx=\frac{\pi}{4},
\]
for every \(b>0\), including the received value \(2\sqrt{2}\). Four times this integral is \(\pi\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi.
\]
