## Problem

\[
\int_0^7\Biggl(\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x^2+1}+x}-\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x^2+1}-x}\Biggr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF sets

\[
y=\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x^2+1}+x}-\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{x^2+1}-x}
\]

and expands \(y^3\) by Cardano's identity. The cross term simplifies to \(y^3=2x-3y\), so

\[
x=\frac{y^3+3y}{2},\qquad dx=\frac32(y^2+1)\,dy.
\]

At \(x=0\), \(y=0\). At \(x=7\), \(y=2\). The integral is

\[
\frac32\int_0^2 y(y^2+1)\,dy=\frac32\Bigl[\frac14 y^4+\frac12 y^2\Bigr]_0^2=9.
\]

## Final answer

\[
9.
\]
