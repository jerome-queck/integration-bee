## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\Biggl(\cos(x)^{\sin(x)^{\cos(x)}}-\sin(x)^{\cos(x)^{\sin(x)}}\Biggr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(x\mapsto\pi/2-x\) swaps \(\sin\) and \(\cos\), so it swaps the two towers. The integrand changes sign and the interval is unchanged, so the integral is \(0\). The mark scheme prints \(0\).
