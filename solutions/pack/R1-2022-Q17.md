## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{\cos t}{\cosh t}\,dt.
\]

## Solution

The integrand is even. On \((0,\infty)\),

\[
\frac{1}{\cosh t}=\frac{2e^{-t}}{1+e^{-2t}}=2e^{-t}\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n e^{-2nt},
\]

so the worked PDF obtains

\[
\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{\cos t}{\cosh t}\,dt
=4\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n\int_0^\infty\cos(t)\,e^{-(2n+1)t}\,dt
=4\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n\frac{2n+1}{(2n+1)^2+1}.
\]

That series equals \(\pi\operatorname{sech}(\pi/2)\). The worked PDF also names a rectangular contour as another route to the same value. The mark scheme prints \(\pi\operatorname{sech}(\pi/2)\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi\operatorname{sech}\frac\pi2.
\]
