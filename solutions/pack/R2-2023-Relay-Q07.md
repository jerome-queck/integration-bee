## Problem

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n^{2}}\int_{0}^{n^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{n^{2}-\lfloor\sqrt{x}\rfloor^{2}}{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On \([m^{2},(m+1)^{2})\) one has \(\lfloor\sqrt{x}\rfloor=m\), and
\[
\int_{m^{2}}^{(m+1)^{2}}x^{-1/2}\,dx=2.
\]
The integral up to \(n^{2}\) is therefore \(2\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\sqrt{n^{2}-m^{2}}\). Dividing by \(n^{2}\) produces the Riemann sum
\[
2\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}\sqrt{1-(m/n)^{2}}\,\frac{1}{n}\to 2\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{1-u^{2}}\,du=\frac{\pi}{2}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{2}.
\]
