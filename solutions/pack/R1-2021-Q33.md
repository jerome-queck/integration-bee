## Problem

\[
\int_0^{2\pi}e^{\cos x}\cos(\sin x)\cos(5x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The integrand is the real part of \(e^{e^{ix}}\cos(5x)\). The exponential series \(e^{e^{ix}}=\sum_{n\ge 0}e^{inx}/n!\) and orthogonality on \([0,2\pi]\) keep only the \(n=5\) cosine:
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{\cos^2(5x)}{5!}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{120}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi/120\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{120}.
\]
