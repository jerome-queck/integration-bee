## Problem

\[
\int_2^4\frac{\sqrt{\ln(9-x)}}{\sqrt{\ln(9-x)}+\sqrt{\ln(x+3)}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f\) be the integrand and set \(I=\int_2^4 f(x)\,dx\). The substitution \(u=6-x\) is the reflection used in the worked PDF. It replaces the numerator \(\sqrt{\ln(9-x)}\) by \(\sqrt{\ln(x+3)}\) and leaves the denominator unchanged, so the reflected integral is again \(I\). Adding the two copies gives

\[
2I=\int_2^4 1\,dx=2,
\]

because the two numerators add up to the common denominator.

## Final answer

1.
