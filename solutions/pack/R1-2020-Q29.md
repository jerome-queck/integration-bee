## Problem

\[
\int\frac{x\ln\bigl(x+\sqrt{x^2+1}\bigr)}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF sets \(x=\sinh\theta\). The integral becomes \(\int\theta\sinh\theta\,d\theta\), and integration by parts gives \(\theta\cosh\theta-\sinh\theta\). Returning to \(x\) produces \(\sqrt{x^2+1}\operatorname{arsinh} x-x\). Since \(\operatorname{arsinh} x=\ln(x+\sqrt{x^2+1})\), this is the answers-sheet form
\[
\sqrt{x^2+1}\ln\bigl(x+\sqrt{x^2+1}\bigr)-x.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\sqrt{x^2+1}\ln\bigl(x+\sqrt{x^2+1}\bigr)-x.
\]
