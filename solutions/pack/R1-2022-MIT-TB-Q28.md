## Problem

\[
\int_1^\infty\frac{1}{x(x^2+1)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Partial fractions give \(1/(x(x^2+1))=1/x-x/(x^2+1)\), with antiderivative \(\ln\bigl(x/\sqrt{x^2+1}\bigr)\). Then
\[
\lim_{b\to\infty}\ln\Bigl(\frac{b}{\sqrt{b^2+1}}\Bigr)-\ln\Bigl(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\Bigr)=\frac{\ln 2}{2}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\ln 2/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\ln 2}{2}.
\]
