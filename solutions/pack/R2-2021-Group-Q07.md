## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\arctan x}{x^{\ln x+1}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(x=e^{u}\). Then \(x^{\ln x}=e^{u^{2}}\) and the integral becomes
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\arctan(e^{u})\,e^{-u^{2}}\,du.
\]
The derivative of \(\arctan(e^{u})+\arctan(e^{-u})\) is \(0\), and the value at \(u=0\) is \(\pi/2\). The sum of the integrand with its reflection is therefore \((\pi/2)e^{-u^{2}}\), and half of that is the even part. The Gaussian integral is \(\sqrt{\pi}\), so the value is
\[
\frac{\pi}{4}\sqrt{\pi}=\frac{\pi^{3/2}}{4}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi^{3/2}/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi^{3/2}}{4}.
\]
