## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\ln(x^{1/b})}{x^{1/b}(x+1)^{2}}\,dx,
\]

## Solution

and pass on the smallest integer greater than the absolute value. Shuttle C2 passes \(b=2\), so the integrand is \(\ln\sqrt{x}/(\sqrt{x}(x+1)^{2})\).

The inherited parameter follows the signed arccot convention consistent with
the printed C1 mark; see the qualification in `R2-2021-Relay-C01.md`.

Set \(x=v^{2}\). The integral becomes
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{2\ln v}{(v^{2}+1)^{2}}\,dv.
\]
For \(a>0\), the substitution \(v=\sqrt{a}\,u\) and the oddness of \(\ln u/(1+u^{2})\) give
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\ln v}{a+v^{2}}\,dv=\frac{\pi\ln a}{4\sqrt{a}}.
\]
Differentiating in \(a\) and setting \(a=1\) produces \(-\pi/4\) for the squared denominator. Twice that is \(-\pi/2\). Since \(1<\pi/2<2\), the smallest integer greater than \(\pi/2\) is \(2\). The mark scheme prints \(2\).

## Final answer

\[
2.
\]
