## Problem

\[
\int_0^\pi\ln(1-2\pi\cos x+\pi^2)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF studies, for \(a>1\),

\[
I(a)=\int_0^\pi\ln(1-2a\cos x+a^2)\,dx
\]

and, after differentiation under the integral sign and the Weierstrass substitution, obtains \(I'(a)=2\pi/a\). Then

\[
I(\pi)-I(1)=\int_1^\pi\frac{2\pi}{a}\,da=2\pi\ln\pi.
\]

The remaining value is

\[
I(1)=\int_0^\pi\ln(2-2\cos x)\,dx=\int_0^\pi\ln\bigl(4\sin^2(x/2)\bigr)\,dx
=\pi\ln 4+4\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin u)\,du.
\]

The log-sine integral equals \(-\frac\pi2\ln 2\), so \(I(1)=\pi\ln 4-2\pi\ln 2=0\). Therefore \(I(\pi)=2\pi\ln\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
2\pi\ln\pi.
\]
