## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}e^{x+e^{x}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The chain rule gives
\[
\frac{d}{dx}e^{e^{x}}=e^{x+e^{x}}.
\]
Evaluating the antiderivative from \(0\) to \(1\),
\[
e^{e^{1}}-e^{e^{0}}=e^{e}-e.
\]

## Final answer

\[
e^{e}-e.
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy differentiates \(e^{e^{x}}\) back to \(e^{x+e^{x}}\), and the endpoint difference is \(e^{e}-e\).
