## Problem

\[\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin(4x)}{\sin x}\,dx.\]

## Solution

The product identity \(\sin(4x)=4\sin x\cos x\cos(2x)\) gives \(\sin(4x)/\sin x=2\bigl(\cos(3x)+\cos x\bigr)\). Then \[\int_{0}^{\pi/2}2\bigl(\cos(3x)+\cos x\bigr)\,dx=2\left[\frac{\sin(3x)}{3}+\sin x\right]_{0}^{\pi/2}=2\left(-\frac13+1\right)=\frac43.\]

The tiebreaker sheet prints \(4/3\).

## Final answer

\[\dfrac{4}{3}.\]
