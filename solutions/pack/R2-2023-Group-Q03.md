## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\det M(x)\,dx,
\]

## Solution

where \(M(x)\) is the \(2024\times 2024\) matrix with ones on the diagonal and \(x\) in the corners \((1,2024)\) and \((2024,1)\).

Rows \(2\) through \(2023\) are standard basis vectors. Expanding along those rows reduces the determinant to the corner block
\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&x\\x&1\end{pmatrix}=1-x^{2}.
\]
The same reduction holds for every size from \(2\) upward. Therefore
\[
\int_{0}^{1}(1-x^{2})\,dx=\frac{2}{3}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(2/3\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{2}{3}.
\]
