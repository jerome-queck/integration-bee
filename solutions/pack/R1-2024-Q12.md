## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(e^{x}+e^{-x}+2)^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The solutions PDF records three routes (hyperbolic Weierstrass, a \(\operatorname{sech}\) reduction, and Camille's substitution). The last one sets \(u=e^{x}\):
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{(e^{x}+e^{-x}+2)^{2}}=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{u}{(u+1)^{4}}\,du=\frac16.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(1/6\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{6}.
\]
