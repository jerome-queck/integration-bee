## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^{2023}-x^{23}}{\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(q>p>0\),
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^{q-1}-x^{p-1}}{\ln x}\,dx=\int_{p}^{q}\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-su}\,du\,ds=\ln\frac{q}{p},
\]
because \(\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-su}\,du=1/s\). Here the exponents are \(2024-1\) and \(24-1\), so the integral is
\[
\ln\frac{2024}{24}=\ln\frac{253}{3}.
\]
The integrand is positive on \((0,1)\). The mark scheme prints both logarithms.

## Final answer

\[
\ln\frac{2024}{24}=\ln\frac{253}{3}.
\]
