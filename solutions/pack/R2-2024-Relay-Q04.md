## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}
\frac{x^{2}}{2\cdot 3-x^{2}+\dfrac{2\cdot 3\,x^{2}}{4\cdot 5-x^{2}+\dfrac{4\cdot 5\,x^{2}}{6\cdot 7-x^{2}+\cdots}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Euler's continued fraction for the sine series rearranges to
\[
\sin x=\frac{x}{1+\dfrac{x^{2}}{2\cdot 3-x^{2}+\dfrac{2\cdot 3\,x^{2}}{4\cdot 5-x^{2}+\cdots}}}.
\]
The denominator of the integrand is exactly that continued fraction, so the integrand is \(x\csc x-1\). A truncation at depth \(40\) agrees with \(x\csc x-1\) to working precision at \(x=1/2\), \(1\), and \(6/5\).

For the definite integral, differentiate \(x\ln\tan(x/2)\):
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\bigl(x\ln\tan(x/2)\bigr)=\ln\tan(x/2)+x\csc x.
\]
The boundary term \(x\ln\tan(x/2)\) tends to \(0\) at both \(0\) and \(\pi/2\). Hence
\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}(x\csc x-1)\,dx=-\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\ln\tan(x/2)\,dx-\frac{\pi}{2}.
\]
The substitution \(u=\tan(x/2)\) gives \(dx=2\,du/(1+u^{2})\) with \(u\) running from \(0\) to \(1\), and
\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\ln\tan(x/2)\,dx=2\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\ln u}{1+u^{2}}\,du=2\sum_{n\ge 0}(-1)^{n}\int_{0}^{1}u^{2n}\ln u\,du.
\]
Each integral equals \(-1/(2n+1)^{2}\), so the series is \(-2G\), where \(G\) is Catalan's constant. The original integral is \(2G-\pi/2\).

## Final answer

\[
2G-\dfrac{\pi}{2}.
\]
