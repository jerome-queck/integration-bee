## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\cosh^{2} x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF recognises the derivative of hyperbolic tangent:
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\tanh x=\operatorname{sech}^{2}x.
\]
Therefore
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\operatorname{sech}^{2}x\,dx=\tanh x\Big|_{-\infty}^{\infty}=1-(-1)=2.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(2\).

## Final answer

\[
2.
\]
