## Problem

Solve
\[
f(x)-\int_{0}^{x}f(t)\,dt=1.
\]

## Solution

The integral vanishes at \(x=0\), so \(f(0)=1\). Differentiating both sides gives \(f'(x)-f(x)=0\). The unique solution is \(f(x)=e^{x}\). Substituting \(e^{x}\) back satisfies the integral equation. The mark scheme prints \(f(x)=e^{x}\).

## Final answer

\[
f(x)=e^{x}.
\]
