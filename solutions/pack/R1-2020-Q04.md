## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\cos(\pi x)-\cos(ex)}{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF introduces
\[
I(a)=\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\cos(\pi x)-\cos(ex)}{x}e^{-ax}\,dx
\]
and differentiates to
\[
I'(a)=\frac{a}{a^{2}+e^{2}}-\frac{a}{a^{2}+\pi^{2}}.
\]
Integrating from \(a\) to \(\infty\), using \(I(\infty)=0\), gives
\[
I(a)=\frac12\ln\Bigl(\frac{a^{2}+e^{2}}{a^{2}+\pi^{2}}\Bigr).
\]
At \(a=0\) this is \(\frac12\ln(e^{2}/\pi^{2})=\ln(e/\pi)\). The worked PDF's last line prints \(\frac12\ln(e/\pi)\). The answers sheet prints \(\ln(e/\pi)\), which is the value of the displayed antiderivative. The answers sheet wins.

## Final answer

\[
\ln\dfrac{e}{\pi}.
\]
