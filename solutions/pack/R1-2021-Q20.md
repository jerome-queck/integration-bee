## Problem

\[
\int\sqrt{x^2-1}\,dx,\qquad x>1.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF sets \(x=\cosh\theta\):

\[
\int\sinh^2\theta\,d\theta=\frac12\int(\cosh 2\theta-1)\,d\theta=\frac12\sinh\theta\cosh\theta-\frac12\theta+C.
\]

Restoring \(x\) gives

\[
\frac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2-1}-\frac12\operatorname{acosh} x+C.
\]

Since \(\operatorname{acosh} x=\ln\bigl(x+\sqrt{x^2-1}\bigr)\) for \(x>1\), this is

\[
\frac12 x\sqrt{x^2-1}-\frac12\ln\bigl|x+\sqrt{x^2-1}\bigr|+C,
\]

which is the antiderivative stacked on the mark scheme.

## Final answer

\[
\frac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2-1}-\frac12\operatorname{acosh} x+C.
\]
