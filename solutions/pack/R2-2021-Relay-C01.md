\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{\sin(\cot^{-1}x)+\cos(\tan^{-1}x)}{x^{2}+1}\,dx,
\]

SymPy's \(\operatorname{acot}\) takes values in \((-\pi/2,\pi/2)\). For \(x<0\) the numerator is \(0\). For \(x>0\) it is \(2/\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\), so the integrand is \(2/(1+x^{2})^{3/2}\). The antiderivative of \((1+x^{2})^{-3/2}\) is \(x/\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\), and
\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{2}{(1+x^{2})^{3/2}}\,dx=\sqrt{2}.
\]
The value passed on is \((\sqrt{2})^{2}-2=0\). The mark scheme prints \(0\).
