## Problem

\[
\int_{-2}^{2}\Bigl(x^{3}\cos\frac{x}{2}+\frac12\Bigr)\sqrt{4-x^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The summand \(x^{3}\cos(x/2)\sqrt{4-x^{2}}\) is odd, so its integral on \([-2,2]\) is \(0\). The remaining integral is half the area of the upper half-disk of radius \(2\):
\[
\frac12\int_{-2}^{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}}\,dx=\frac12\cdot 2\pi=\pi.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi.
