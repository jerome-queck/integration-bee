## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi}\arctan\bigl(3^{\cos x}\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(z=3^{\cos x}>0\). Then \(3^{\cos(\pi-x)}=1/z\), and \(\arctan z+\arctan(1/z)\) has derivative \(0\) with value \(\pi/2\) at \(z=1\). Pairing \(x\) with \(\pi-x\) gives
\[
2I=\int_0^{\pi}\frac{\pi}{2}\,dx=\frac{\pi^{2}}{2},
\]
so \(I=\pi^{2}/4\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi^{2}/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi^{2}}{4}.
\]
