## Problem

\[
\int\frac{\ln(\ln x)}{x\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=\ln(\ln x)\). Then \(du=dx/(x\ln x)\), so the integral is \(u^{2}/2\). The mark scheme prints \(\frac12\ln^{2}(\ln x)\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac12\bigl(\ln(\ln x)\bigr)^{2}.
\]
