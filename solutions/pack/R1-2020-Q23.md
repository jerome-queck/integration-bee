## Problem

\[
\int_0^1 x\left\{\frac1x\right\}\left\lfloor\frac1x\right\rfloor\,dx,
\]

where \(\{y\}=y-\lfloor y\rfloor\).

## Solution

The substitution \(t=1/x\) from the worked PDF produces

\[
\int_1^\infty\frac{\{t\}\,\lfloor t\rfloor}{t^3}\,dt.
\]

On each interval \([n,n+1)\), one has \(\lfloor t\rfloor=n\) and \(\{t\}=t-n\), so the piece is

\[
n\int_n^{n+1}\bigl(t^{-2}-n t^{-3}\bigr)\,dt=\frac{1}{2(n+1)^2}.
\]

Summing from \(n=1\) to \(\infty\),

\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{2(n+1)^2}=\frac12\Bigl(\frac{\pi^2}{6}-1\Bigr)=\frac{\pi^2}{12}-\frac12.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi^2}{12}-\frac12.
\]
