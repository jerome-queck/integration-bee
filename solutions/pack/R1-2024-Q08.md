## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(x+1)(\ln(x)^{2}+1)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(x=1/u\) in the solutions PDF returns
\[
I=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{u(u+1)(\ln(u)^{2}+1)}\,du=\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{u}-\frac{1}{u+1}\right)\frac{du}{\ln(u)^{2}+1}.
\]
The second piece is \(I\) again, so
\[
2I=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x(\ln(x)^{2}+1)}.
\]
Set \(y=\ln x\). Then \(2I=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}dy/(y^{2}+1)=\pi\), and \(I=\pi/2\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{2}.
\]
