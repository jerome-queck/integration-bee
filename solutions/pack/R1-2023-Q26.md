## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\arctan x}{x^{4/3}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF integrates by parts. The boundary term \(-3x^{-1/3}\arctan x\) vanishes at both \(0\) and \(\infty\), and
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\arctan x}{x^{4/3}}\,dx=3\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{-1/3}}{1+x^{2}}\,dx.
\]
The substitution \(x^{2}=t\) turns the right-hand side into
\[
\frac32\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{t^{1/3-1}}{1+t}\,dt=\frac32\,B\left(\frac13,\frac23\right)=\frac32\cdot\frac{\pi}{\sin(\pi/3)}=\pi\sqrt{3}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi\sqrt{3}\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi\sqrt{3}.
\]
