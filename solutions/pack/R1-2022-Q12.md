## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\infty}\cos(x^{2})\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF introduces \(I(t)=\int_0^{\infty}\cos(t x^{2})\,dx\) for \(t>0\) and evaluates it by a Laplace transform. The scaling \(u=x\sqrt{t}\) gives
\[
I(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{t}}\int_0^{\infty}\cos(u^{2})\,du,
\]
and the transform inverts to \(I(t)=\sqrt{\pi/(8t)}\). At \(t=1\),
\[
\int_0^{\infty}\cos(x^{2})\,dx=\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{8}}=\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{4}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\frac12\sqrt{\pi/2}\), which is the same value.

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{4}.
\]
