## Problem

\[
\int\sqrt{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[4]{x\sqrt[5]{x\cdots}}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF separates the nested radicals:

\[
x^{1/2}\cdot x^{1/(2\cdot 3)}\cdot x^{1/(2\cdot 3\cdot 4)}\cdots
=x^{\sum_{k=2}^\infty 1/k!}.
\]

The exponent is \(e-2\), so the integrand is \(x^{e-2}\) and the antiderivative is the mark-scheme expression.

## Final answer

\[
\frac{x^{e-1}}{e-1}+C.
\]
