## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^{3}+x+1}{3x^{2}-3x+4}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The denominator is unchanged by \(u=1-x\). The worked PDF applies that substitution to the numerator:
\[
(1-x)^{3}+(1-x)+1=3-4x+3x^{2}-x^{3}.
\]
Adding this to \(x^{3}+x+1\) produces exactly \(3x^{2}-3x+4\). Therefore \(2I=\int_{0}^{1}1\,dx=1\), and \(I=1/2\). The mark scheme prints \(1/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{2}.
\]
