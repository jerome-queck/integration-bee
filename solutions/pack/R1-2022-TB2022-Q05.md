## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\ln\Bigl(\frac{1+x}{1-x}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The antiderivative is \((1+x)\ln(1+x)+(1-x)\ln(1-x)\), because the extra \(\pm 1\) terms cancel. Its limit from \(0\) to \(1\) is \(2\ln 2=\ln 4\). The mark scheme prints \(\ln 4\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 4.
\]
