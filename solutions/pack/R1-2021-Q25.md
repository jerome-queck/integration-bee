## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\arctan x}{x\bigl((\ln x)^2+1\bigr)}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(u=1/x\) preserves \(dx/x\). For \(u>0\), \(\arctan u+\arctan(1/u)=\pi/2\), so the original integral \(I\) and its reflected copy add to
\[
2I=\frac\pi2\int_0^\infty\frac{dx}{x\bigl((\ln x)^2+1\bigr)}.
\]
The further substitution \(t=\ln x\) evaluates the right-hand side:
\[
I=\frac\pi4\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{dt}{t^2+1}=\frac\pi4\cdot\pi=\frac{\pi^2}{4}.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\pi^2/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi^2}{4}.
\]
