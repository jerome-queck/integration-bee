## Problem

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{5/6}^{\infty}e^{-x^{n}}\,dx,
\]

## Solution

The question asks to write this value as a reduced fraction \(p/q\) and pass on the prime factors of \(q\).

For \(5/6\le x<1\), \(x^{n}\to 0\), so the integrand tends to \(1\). For \(x>1\), \(x^{n}\to\infty\) and the integrand tends to \(0\), dominated by the integrable function \(e^{-x}\). The limit is the length of \([5/6,1]\),
\[
\frac{1}{6}.
\]
The prime factors of \(6\) are \(2\) and \(3\). The mark scheme accepts \(2,3\) or \(3,2\).

## Final answer

\[
2,3.
\]
