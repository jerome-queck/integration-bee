## Problem

\[
\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{x}{\lfloor x\rfloor^{4}+4}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Split at the integers:
\[
\sum_{n\ge 1}\int_{n}^{n+1}\frac{x}{n^{4}+4}\,dx=\frac12\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{2n+1}{n^{4}+4}.
\]
Sophie Germain's identity factors \(n^{4}+4=(n^{2}+2n+2)(n^{2}-2n+2)\). Partial fractions give
\[
\frac{2n+1}{(n^{2}+2n+2)(n^{2}-2n+2)}=\frac18\Biggl(\frac{n-2}{n^{2}+2n+2}-\frac{n-6}{n^{2}-2n+2}\Biggr).
\]
Rewriting the first numerator as \((n+2)-4\) produces a telescoping piece whose sum is \(4\), together with
\[
\frac14\sum_{n\ge 1}\frac{1}{n^{2}-2n+2}=\frac14\sum_{m\ge 0}\frac{1}{m^{2}+1}.
\]
The \(m=0\) term is \(1\), and
\[
\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{1}{m^{2}+1}=\frac{\pi}{2}\coth\pi-\frac12,
\]
so the \(m\ge 0\) sum is \(\pi\coth\pi/2+1/2\). Assembling the factors,
\[
\frac12\Biggl(\frac18\cdot 4+\frac14\Bigl(\frac{\pi}{2}\coth\pi+\frac12\Bigr)\Biggr)=\frac{5+\pi\coth\pi}{16}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{5+\pi\coth\pi}{16}.
\]
