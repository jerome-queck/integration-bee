## Problem

\[
\int_0^1 x^{1/\ln x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(x\in(0,1)\) with \(x\neq 1\),
\[
x^{1/\ln x}=\exp\Bigl(\frac{\ln x}{\ln x}\Bigr)=e.
\]
The value at the single point \(x=1\) does not affect the integral, and the integrand stays bounded near \(0\) because it is the constant \(e\). Therefore
\[
\int_0^1 x^{1/\ln x}\,dx=\int_0^1 e\,dx=e.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(e\).

e.
