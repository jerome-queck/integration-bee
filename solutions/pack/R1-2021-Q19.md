## Problem

\[
\int_0^{2\pi}e^{3\cos x}\cos(3\sin x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The integrand is the real part of a complex exponential:
\[
e^{3\cos x}\cos(3\sin x)=\operatorname{Re}\bigl(e^{3e^{ix}}\bigr).
\]
Expand the exponential and integrate termwise, as in the worked PDF:
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}e^{3e^{ix}}\,dx=\int_0^{2\pi}\sum_{n\ge 0}\frac{3^{n}}{n!}e^{inx}\,dx=2\pi,
\]
because every positive integer frequency integrates to \(0\) over a full period. The integral is real, so the original value is \(2\pi\). The mark scheme prints \(2\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
2\pi.
\]
