## Problem

\[
\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{(x-1)^{3}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(u=\sqrt{x-1}\), so \(x-1=u^{2}\) and \(dx=2u\,du\). The denominator is \(u+u^{3}=u(1+u^{2})\), and
\[
\int\frac{2u\,du}{u(1+u^{2})}=\int\frac{2\,du}{1+u^{2}}=2\arctan u+C.
\]
Equivalently, \(\sqrt{x-1}+(x-1)^{3/2}=x\sqrt{x-1}\). The answers sheet prints \(2\arctan\sqrt{x-1}+C\).

## Final answer

\[
2\arctan\sqrt{x-1}+C.
\]
