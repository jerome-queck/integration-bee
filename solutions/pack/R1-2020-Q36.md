## Problem

\[
\int\frac{x}{x^{4}+4}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF prints the antiderivative
\[
\frac14\arctan\frac{x^{2}}{2}+C.
\]
Differentiating gives
\[
\frac14\cdot\frac{1}{1+(x^{2}/2)^{2}}\cdot x=\frac{x}{x^{4}+4}.
\]
The answers sheet prints the same arctangent.

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{1}{4}\arctan\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}+C.
\]
