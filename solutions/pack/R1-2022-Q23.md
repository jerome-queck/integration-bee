## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\Bigl(\frac1x-1\Bigr)^{1/4}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

\[
\Bigl(\frac1x-1\Bigr)^{1/4}=x^{-1/4}(1-x)^{1/4},
\]

so the integral is the beta value

\[
B\Bigl(\frac34,\frac54\Bigr)=\frac{\Gamma(3/4)\Gamma(5/4)}{\Gamma(2)}=\frac14\Gamma\Bigl(\frac14\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\frac34\Bigr).
\]

The reflection formula \(\Gamma(z)\Gamma(1-z)=\pi/\sin(\pi z)\) at \(z=1/4\) gives \(\Gamma(1/4)\Gamma(3/4)=\pi/\sin(\pi/4)=\pi\sqrt{2}\). Hence

\[
\frac14\cdot\pi\sqrt{2}=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{2}}.
\]

The worked PDF writes the parameters as \(B(5/4,3/4)\), which is the same number.

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{2}}.
\]
