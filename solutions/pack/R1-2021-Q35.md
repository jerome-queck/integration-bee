## Problem

\[
\int_0^e W(x)\,dx,
\]
where \(W\) is the Lambert \(W\) function, the solution of \(W(x)e^{W(x)}=x\).

## Solution

Set \(u=W(x)\), so \(x=ue^u\) and \(dx=(u+1)e^u\,du\). The endpoints \(x=0\) and \(x=e\) become \(u=0\) and \(u=1\). The integral is
\[
\int_0^1 u(u+1)e^u\,du=\int_0^1(u^2+u)e^u\,du=\bigl[(u^2-u+1)e^u\bigr]_0^1=e-1.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(e-1\).

## Final answer

\[
e-1.
\]
