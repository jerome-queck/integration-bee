## Problem

\[
\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{1}{3^{x}+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=1/(3^{x}+1)\). The substitution \(u=-x\) in the worked PDF leaves the integral unchanged and replaces \(f(x)\) by \(f(-x)\). Adding the two copies,
\[
f(x)+f(-x)=\frac{1}{3^{x}+1}+\frac{3^{x}}{3^{x}+1}=1,
\]
so \(2I=\int_{-1}^{1}1\,dx=2\) and \(I=1\). The mark scheme prints \(1\).
