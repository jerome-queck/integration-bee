## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\arctan x\,\ln(1+x^2)}{x(a^2+x^2)}\,dx,\qquad a>0.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF uses \(\arctan x=(i/2)(\ln(1-ix)-\ln(1+ix))\) and \(\ln(1+x^2)=\ln(1+ix)+\ln(1-ix)\), extends the integrand to \(\mathbb{R}\) by evenness, and closes one logarithm in the upper half-plane and the other in the lower half-plane. The big arcs vanish, and \(z=0\) is removable. The only enclosed poles are \(z=ia\) and \(z=-ia\):

\[
\operatorname{Res}_{z=ia}\frac{\ln^2(1-iz)}{z(z^2+a^2)}=\frac{\ln^2(1+a)}{-2a^2},
\]
\[
\operatorname{Res}_{z=-ia}\frac{\ln^2(1+iz)}{z(z^2+a^2)}=\frac{\ln^2(1+a)}{-2a^2}.
\]

The prefactor \(i/4\) on the first integral and \(-i/4\) on the second, each multiplied by the orientation \(2\pi i\) or \(-2\pi i\), add to

\[
\frac{\pi\ln^2(1+a)}{2a^2}.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi\ln^2(1+a)}{2a^2}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

`verify/pack_handoff_e_parameter.py` checks the literal integrand, endpoints,
and the explicit positive assumption on \(a\). The logarithm identities are
checked by their derivatives and their value at zero. In the upper/lower
half-planes used above, \(\Re(1\mp iz)=1+|\Im z|\ge1\), so the principal
logarithms have no branch cut or zero inside the contours. SymPy checks the
removable singularity at zero, both residues, the contour orientations, and
the vanishing bound

\[
\left|\int_{\text{semicircle}}\frac{\ln^2(1\mp iz)}{z(z^2+a^2)}\,dz\right|
\le \frac{\pi\bigl(\ln(1+R)+\pi/2\bigr)^2}{R^2-a^2}
\longrightarrow0 \qquad (R>a).
\]

The gate derives the result from the residue combination and compares it
with the independently parsed mark; numeric agreement is not used.
