## Problem

\[
\int_0^\infty\bigl(-\{x\}\bigr)^{\lfloor x\rfloor}\,dx,
\]

where \(\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor\).

## Solution

Split at the integers. On \([n,n+1)\) the integrand is \((-\{x\})^n\), and \(\{x\}=x-n\), so

\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1)^n\int_0^1 t^n\,dt=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n}{n+1}=\ln 2.
\]

The series is the Taylor expansion of \(\ln(1+u)\) at \(u=1\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 2.
\]
