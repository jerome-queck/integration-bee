## Problem

\[
\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^{420}(x)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF reduces the full period to a quarter period and evaluates the Wallis integral through the beta function. For the even power \(420\),

\[
\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^{420}x\,dx=4\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^{420}x\,dx=2\,B\Bigl(\tfrac12,210+\tfrac12\Bigr)
=2\frac{\Gamma(1/2)\,\Gamma(421/2)}{\Gamma(211)}.
\]

Legendre's duplication formula \(\Gamma(z)\Gamma(z+1/2)=2^{1-2z}\sqrt{\pi}\,\Gamma(2z)\) at \(z=210\) gives

\[
\Gamma(421/2)=2^{1-420}\sqrt{\pi}\,\frac{\Gamma(420)}{\Gamma(210)}.
\]

With \(\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt{\pi}\), \(\Gamma(420)=419!\), \(\Gamma(210)=209!\), and \(\Gamma(211)=210!\), this is

\[
2\cdot 2^{1-420}\pi\cdot\frac{419!}{210!\,209!}=\frac{\pi}{2^{419}}\binom{420}{210}.
\]

The same value is \(2\pi\cdot 420!/\bigl(2^{420}(210!)^2\bigr)\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi}{2^{419}}\binom{420}{210}.
\]
