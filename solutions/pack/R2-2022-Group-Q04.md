## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{(\sin x+\cos x)^{2}}.
\]

## Solution

The denominator is \(2\sin^{2}(x+\pi/4)\), so the integral is
\[
\frac12\int_{\pi/4}^{3\pi/4}\csc^{2}u\,du=\frac12\bigl[-\cot u\bigr]_{\pi/4}^{3\pi/4}=\frac12\bigl(1+1\bigr)=1.
\]

## Final answer

\[
1.
