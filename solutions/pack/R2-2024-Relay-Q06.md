## Problem

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}n\int_{-1/20}^{1/24}\bigl(1-|x|+|x|^{3}\bigr)^{n}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Put \(y=nx\). The expression becomes
\[
\int_{-n/20}^{n/24}\Bigl(1-\frac{|y|}{n}+\frac{|y|^{3}}{n^{3}}\Bigr)^{n}\,dy.
\]
For each fixed \(y\),
\[
n\ln\Bigl(1-\frac{|y|}{n}+\frac{|y|^{3}}{n^{3}}\Bigr)\to -|y|,
\]
so the integrand tends to \(e^{-|y|}\). On the original interval one has \(|x|\le 1/20\), hence \(|x|^{3}\le |x|/400\) and the base is at most \(1-c|x|\) for a positive \(c\). After the change of variables the integrands are dominated by an integrable multiple of \(e^{-c|y|}\). Dominated convergence gives
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-|y|}\,dy=2.
\]

## Final answer

\[
2.
\]
