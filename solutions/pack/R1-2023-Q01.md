## Problem

\[
\int_{-1}^{1}\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF sets \(y=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\). Then \(x^{2}+y^{2}=1\) with \(y\ge 0\), so the integrand is the upper semicircle of radius \(1\). The integral is the area of that semicircle. The mark scheme prints \(\pi/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{2}.
\]
