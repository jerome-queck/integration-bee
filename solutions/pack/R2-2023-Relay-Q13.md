## Problem

For a fixed positive integer \(n\),
\[
\lim_{x\to 0}\int_{x}^{2x}\frac{\tan^{n-1}x}{\sin^{n}x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The printed integrand uses the same letter as the outer limit. The mark scheme prints the constant \(\ln 2\), which is the value obtained by reading that letter as the variable of integration, in the same way as the 2022 relay limit \(\int_{x}^{2x}\sin^{n-1}t/t^{n}\,dt\). With that reading, set the integration variable equal to \(xv\). Then \(v\) runs from \(1\) to \(2\) and
\[
\int_{1}^{2}\Biggl(\frac{\tan(xv)}{xv}\Biggr)^{n-1}\frac{1}{v}\,dv.
\]
As \(x\to 0\), \(\tan(xv)/(xv)\to 1\) uniformly on \([1,2]\), and the integral tends to \(\int_{1}^{2}dv/v=\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 2.
\]
