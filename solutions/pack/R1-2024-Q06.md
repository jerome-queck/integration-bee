## Problem

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{-x^{2}}}{e^{x}+1}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=e^{-x^{2}}/(e^{x}+1)\). The solutions PDF substitutes \(x=-u\):
\[
f(x)+f(-x)=e^{-x^{2}}.
\]
Therefore
\[
2I=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^{2}}\,dx=\sqrt{\pi},
\]
and \(I=\sqrt{\pi}/2\). The mark scheme prints \(\sqrt{\pi}/2\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}.
\]
