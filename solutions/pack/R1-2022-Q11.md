## Problem

\[
\int_0^{1/4}\sum_{n=0}^\infty\binom{2n}{n}x^n\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF identifies the generating function

\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\binom{2n}{n}x^n=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-4x}},\qquad |x|<\frac14.
\]

At the endpoint \(x=1/4\) the series diverges, and the improper integral still converges. The derivative of \(-\frac12(1-4x)^{1/2}\) is \((1-4x)^{-1/2}\), so

\[
\int_0^{1/4}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-4x}}=\frac12.
\]

## Final answer

\[
\frac12.
\]
