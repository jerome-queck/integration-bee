## Problem

\[
\int\frac{x\arcsin x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The worked PDF sets \(x=\sin\theta\), so the integral becomes \(\int\theta\sin\theta\,d\theta\). Integration by parts gives
\[
-\theta\cos\theta+\sin\theta+C=x-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\arcsin x+C.
\]
The mark scheme prints the same antiderivative.

## Final answer

\[
x-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\arcsin x+C.
\]
