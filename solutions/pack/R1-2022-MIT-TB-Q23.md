## Problem

\[
\int\bigl(\ln(x^{2})-2\ln(2x)\bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The integrand simplifies before any integration:
\[
\ln(x^{2})-2\ln(2x)=2\ln x-2\ln 2-2\ln x=-2\ln 2.
\]
An antiderivative is \(-2x\ln 2\). The mark scheme prints \(-2x\log 2\), and \(\log\) is the natural logarithm.

## Final answer

\[
-2x\ln 2.
\]
