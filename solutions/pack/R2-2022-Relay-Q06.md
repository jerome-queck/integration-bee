## Problem

\[
\int_{1}^{3}\frac{\ln x}{x^{2}+3}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(x=\sqrt{3}\,u\). The interval \([1,3]\) becomes \([1/\sqrt{3},\sqrt{3}]\), and
\[
\int_{1/\sqrt{3}}^{\sqrt{3}}\frac{\tfrac12\ln 3+\ln u}{\sqrt{3}\,(u^{2}+1)}\,du.
\]
The coefficient of \(\ln 3\) is
\[
\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\cdot\frac{\ln 3}{2}\cdot\Biggl(\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{\pi}{6}\Biggr)=\frac{\pi\ln 3}{12\sqrt{3}}.
\]
The remaining integral of \(\ln u/(u^{2}+1)\) vanishes: the substitution \(u=1/v\) returns the same integrand, and reversing the limits shows that the integral equals its negative. The mark scheme prints \(\pi\ln 3/(12\sqrt{3})\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{\pi\ln 3}{12\sqrt{3}}.
\]
