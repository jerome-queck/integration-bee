## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin x\cos x\,(\cos x-\sin x)\,dx.
\]

The problem line in the solutions PDF is the product \(\cos x\sin x\cos x-\sin x\cos x\sin x\), which is this integrand.

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=\sin x\cos x(\cos x-\sin x)\). The reflection \(x\mapsto\pi/2-x\) sends \(f\) to \(-f\). The worked PDF therefore reads the integral as zero by the King property: the contributions of \(f\) and its reflection cancel on \([0,\pi/2]\).
