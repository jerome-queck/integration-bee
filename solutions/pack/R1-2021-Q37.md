## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\Bigl(\cos(x)^{\sin(x)^{\cos x}}-\sin(x)^{\cos(x)^{\sin x}}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Let \(f(x)=\cos(x)^{\sin(x)^{\cos x}}-\sin(x)^{\cos(x)^{\sin x}}\). The reflection \(x\mapsto\pi/2-x\) swaps the two towers, so \(f(\pi/2-x)=-f(x)\). On \([0,\pi/2]\) the King property gives \(\int f=0\). A product such as \(\sin x\cos x(\cos x-\sin x)\) is a different integrand; it is not this nested difference.
