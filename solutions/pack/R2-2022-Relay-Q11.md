## Problem

For a fixed positive integer \(n\),
\[
\lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\int_{x}^{2x}\frac{\sin^{n-1}t}{t^{n}}\,dt.
\]

## Solution

Set \(t=xv\), so \(v\) runs from \(1\) to \(2\) and
\[
\int_{1}^{2}\Biggl(\frac{\sin(xv)}{xv}\Biggr)^{n-1}\frac{1}{v}\,dv.
\]
As \(x\downarrow 0\), the quotient \(\sin(xv)/(xv)\) tends to \(1\) uniformly for \(v\in[1,2]\). The limit is
\[
\int_{1}^{2}\frac{dv}{v}=\ln 2.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 2.
\]
