## Problem

\[
\int_{2}^{\infty}\ln\Biggl(\frac{\lfloor x\rfloor^{2}-1}{\lfloor x\rfloor^{2}}\Biggr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

On each interval \([n,n+1)\) with an integer \(n\ge 2\), the floor is \(n\), so the integrand is the constant
\[
\ln\Biggl(1-\frac{1}{n^{2}}\Biggr)=\ln(n-1)+\ln(n+1)-2\ln n.
\]
The partial integral up to \(N+1\) is therefore
\[
\sum_{n=2}^{N}\bigl(\ln(n-1)-\ln n\bigr)+\sum_{n=2}^{N}\bigl(\ln(n+1)-\ln n\bigr).
\]
The first sum is \(-\ln N\). The second is \(\ln(N+1)-\ln 2\). As \(N\to\infty\) the combination tends to \(-\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
-\ln 2.
\]
