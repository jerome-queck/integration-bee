## Problem

How many solutions does
\[
\int_{0}^{x}\sin(\sin t)\,dt=0
\]
have for \(0<x\le 2\pi\)?

## Solution

Let \(F(x)=\int_{0}^{x}\sin(\sin t)\,dt\). Then \(F'(x)=\sin(\sin x)\). On \((0,\pi)\), \(\sin x>0\), so \(F'>0\). On \((\pi,2\pi)\), \(\sin x<0\), so \(F'<0\). Also
\[
\sin(\sin(t+\pi))=\sin(-\sin t)=-\sin(\sin t),
\]
hence \(F(2\pi)=0\). Starting from \(F(0)=0\), \(F\) rises to a positive value and returns to \(0\) only at the right endpoint. There is one root in \((0,2\pi]\). The mark scheme prints \(1\).

## Final answer

\[
1.
\]
