## Problem

\[
\int_0^{\infty}\frac{1}{\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)^{2}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Clear the inner sum:
\[
\left(x+\frac1x\right)^{2}=\frac{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}{x^{2}},
\qquad
\frac{1}{\left(x+1/x\right)^{2}}=\frac{x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}.
\]
The worked PDF splits the rational function as
\[
\frac{x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}=\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}-\frac{1}{(1+x^{2})^{2}}.
\]
The substitution \(x=\tan\theta\) turns the integrand into \(\sin^{2}\theta\) on \([0,\pi/2]\), and
\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^{2}\theta\,d\theta=\frac\pi4.
\]
The answers sheet prints \(\pi/4\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{4}.
\]
