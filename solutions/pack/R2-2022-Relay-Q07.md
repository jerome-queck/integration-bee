## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{\sinh x-x}{x^{2}\sinh x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The integrand is \((1-x\operatorname{csch} x)/x^{2}\). For \(x>0\),
\[
\operatorname{csch} x=\frac{1}{x}+2x\sum_{k\ge 1}\frac{(-1)^{k}}{x^{2}+\pi^{2}k^{2}},
\]
so
\[
\frac{1-x\operatorname{csch} x}{x^{2}}=2\sum_{k\ge 1}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{x^{2}+\pi^{2}k^{2}}.
\]
Each term integrates to
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{2}+\pi^{2}k^{2}}=\frac{1}{2k}.
\]
The series collapses to \(\sum_{k\ge 1}(-1)^{k-1}/k=\ln 2\). The mark scheme prints \(\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 2.
\]
