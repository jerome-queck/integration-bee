## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\ln\Bigl(\frac{1+x}{1-x}\Bigr)\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The derivative of \(F(x)=(1+x)\ln(1+x)+(1-x)\ln(1-x)\) is the integrand. As \(x\to 1^-\), \((1-x)\ln(1-x)\to 0\), so
\[
\lim_{x\to 1^-}F(x)-F(0)=2\ln 2=\ln 4.
\]
The mark scheme prints \(\ln 4\).

## Final answer

\[
\ln 4.
\]
