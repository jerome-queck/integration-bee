## Problem

\[
\int_0^1\frac{1}{1-x}\sqrt{\frac{\{1/x\}}{1-\{1/x\}}}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(x=1/t\) and the splitting \(1/(t(t-1))=1/(t-1)-1/t\), as written in the worked PDF, produce

\[
\int_1^\infty\Biggl(\frac{1}{t-1}-\frac{1}{t}\Biggr)\sqrt{\frac{\{t\}}{1-\{t\}}}\,dt.
\]

The two pieces \(1/(t-1)\) and \(1/t\) are separately divergent. On each finite interval \([n,n+1]\) their difference is the convergent combination \(1/(t(t-1))\). Shift that combined integrand by \(1\). Periodicity of the fractional part then leaves

\[
\int_0^1\frac{1}{t}\sqrt{\frac{t}{1-t}}\,dt=\int_0^1\frac{dt}{\sqrt{t}\,\sqrt{1-t}}.
\]

The substitution \(t=\sin^2\theta\) gives \(2\int_0^{\pi/2}1\,d\theta=\pi\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi.
\]
