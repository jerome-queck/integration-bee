## Problem

\[
\int e^{x}\,de.
\]
The differential is \(de\), so \(e\) is the variable of integration and \(x\) is constant.

## Solution

Differentiating with respect to the variable \(e\),
\[
\frac{d}{de}\frac{e^{x+1}}{x+1}=e^{x}.
\]
The antiderivative is \(e^{x+1}/(x+1)\), up to a constant. The mark scheme prints \(e^{x+1}/(x+1)\).

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{e^{x+1}}{x+1}.
\]

## SymPy self-check

With \(e\) and \(x\) as positive symbols, SymPy differentiates \(e^{x+1}/(x+1)\) in \(e\) and gets \(e^{x}\).
