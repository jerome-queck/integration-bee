## Problem

\[
\int_{1/4}^{4}\frac{\arctan x}{x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

The substitution \(u=1/x\) reverses the limits \(1/4\) and \(4\) and replaces \(\arctan x\) by \(\arctan(1/x)\). Adding the two copies, and using \(\arctan x+\arctan(1/x)=\pi/2\) for \(x>0\),
\[
2I=\int_{1/4}^{4}\frac{\pi/2}{x}\,dx=\frac{\pi}{2}\ln 16=\pi\ln 4.
\]
Hence \(I=\pi\ln 2\). The mark scheme prints \(\pi\ln 2\).

## Final answer

\[
\pi\ln 2.
\]
