## Problem

\[
\int\frac{2023^x}{2023^x+2024^x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Divide by \(2024^x\):

\[
\int\frac{(2023/2024)^x}{1+(2023/2024)^x}\,dx
=\frac{\ln\bigl(1+(2023/2024)^x\bigr)}{\ln(2023/2024)}+C.
\]

The worked PDF rewrites the same antiderivative as

\[
x-\frac{\ln\bigl(1+(2024/2023)^x\bigr)}{\ln(2024/2023)}+C.
\]

The mark scheme prints the second expression. On the reals the two differ by a constant.

## Final answer

\[
x-\frac{\ln\bigl(1+(2024/2023)^x\bigr)}{\ln(2024/2023)}+C.
\]
