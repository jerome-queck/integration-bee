## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/4}\sqrt{\tan x}\,\sqrt{1-\tan x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Set \(t=\tan x\), so the integral is \(\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{t(1-t)}/(1+t^{2})\,dt\). The further substitution \(t=1/(1+u^{2})\) produces
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{2u^{2}}{(1+u^{2})(u^{4}+2u^{2}+2)}\,du.
\]
Partial fractions split the integrand into \(-2/(u^{2}+1)\) and \((2u^{2}+4)/(u^{4}+2u^{2}+2)\). The first piece contributes \(-\pi\) on \([0,\infty)\). The quartic piece has an elementary antiderivative, and denesting its value cancels the extra radicals against
\[
\pi\Bigl(\frac{\sqrt{2+2\sqrt{2}}}{2}-1\Bigr).
\]
The mark scheme prints that expression.

## Final answer

\[
\pi\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt{2+2\sqrt{2}}}{2}-1\Bigr).
\]
