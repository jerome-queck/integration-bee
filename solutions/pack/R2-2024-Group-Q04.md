## Problem

\[
\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\arctan(\sin x)}{\sin x}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

For \(a\ge 0\) set
\[
I(a)=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\arctan(a\sin x)}{\sin x}\,dx.
\]
Differentiating under the integral sign,
\[
I'(a)=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{1+a^{2}\sin^{2}x}=\int_{0}^{\pi/2}\frac{\csc^{2}x}{\cot^{2}x+a^{2}+1}\,dx.
\]
The substitution \(u=\cot x\) runs from \(\infty\) down to \(0\) and gives
\[
I'(a)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{du}{u^{2}+a^{2}+1}=\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{a^{2}+1}}.
\]
Then \(I(0)=0\), so \(I(a)=(\pi/2)\operatorname{arsinh} a\). At \(a=1\),
\[
I(1)=\frac{\pi}{2}\operatorname{arsinh} 1=\frac{\pi}{2}\ln(1+\sqrt{2}).
\]
The mark scheme prints that logarithm.

## Final answer

\[
\dfrac{\pi}{2}\ln(1+\sqrt{2}).
\]

## SymPy self-check

SymPy simplifies
\[
\frac{1}{1+a^{2}\sin^{2}x}-\frac{\csc^{2}x}{\cot^{2}x+a^{2}+1}
\]
to \(0\), and evaluates \(\int_{0}^{\infty}du/(u^{2}+a^{2}+1)=\pi/(2\sqrt{a^{2}+1})\) for \(a>0\).
