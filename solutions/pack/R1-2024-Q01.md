## Problem

The bar denotes the average on the interval of integration:

\[
\frac{1}{2}\int_{-1}^{1} e^{in}\,dn.
\]

## Solution

The solutions PDF evaluates the average directly:

\[
\frac{1}{2}\int_{-1}^{1} e^{in}\,dn=\frac{e^{i}-e^{-i}}{2i}=\sin 1.
\]
