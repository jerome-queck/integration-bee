## Problem

\[
\int\frac{(3x^{10}+2x^8-2)\,(x^{10}+x^8+1)^{1/4}}{x^6}\,dx.
\]

## Solution

Factor \(x^{10}+x^8+1=x^4(x^6+x^4+x^{-4})\). The fourth root contributes a factor of \(x\), and dividing by \(x^6\) leaves

\[
\bigl(3x^5+2x^3-2x^{-5}\bigr)\,(x^6+x^4+x^{-4})^{1/4}.
\]

Set \(u=x^6+x^4+x^{-4}\). Then

\[
\frac{du}{dx}=6x^5+4x^3-4x^{-5}=2\bigl(3x^5+2x^3-2x^{-5}\bigr),
\]

so the integrand is \(\frac12 u^{1/4}\,du/dx\). Integrating gives \(\frac12\cdot\frac45 u^{5/4}\).

## Final answer

\[
\frac{2\,(x^{10}+x^8+1)^{5/4}}{5x^5}+C.
\]

The 2020 answers sheet prints \(\frac{2}{5}(x^6+x^4+x^{-4})^{5/4}+C\). That form equals the answer above for \(x>0\). For \(x<0\) the factoring step should read \((x^{10}+x^8+1)^{1/4}=|x|\,(x^6+x^4+x^{-4})^{1/4}\), so the printed form is even while the integrand needs an odd antiderivative. The stored answer is valid on both sides of \(0\).
