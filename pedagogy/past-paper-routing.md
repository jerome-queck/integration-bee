# Past-paper routing

A Round 1 paper is an index. The lesson is a coach card and a unit drill. This note maps a common integrand shape to that card and that unit. It does not quote a pack final, a Yang practice final, or a per-question tag. The paper PDFs stay closed while the lesson is the shape.

Open the earlier row when two shapes fit. Stop when the integral is finished. The dose is `pedagogy/round1-coach.md`. A format blank, a digit, a bank decision, or a boxed line is not a new shape. Those open `docs/techniques/formats.md`.

| Shape on the page | Card | Unit drill |
|---|---|---|
| A factor that is a constant times a derivative, a pure power, or \(e^{ax}\) | `docs/techniques/concepts/u-substitution.md` | Unit 01, then 06 |
| \(x e^{ax}\), \(x\sin x\), \(x\cos x\), or \(\ln x\) | `docs/techniques/concepts/integration-by-parts.md` | Unit 03, then 15 |
| An odd or even integrand on an interval symmetric about 0 | `docs/techniques/concepts/symmetry-kings.md` | Unit 02 |
| A reflection \(x\mapsto a+b-x\) | the same card | Unit 10, then 25 |
| An absolute value | `docs/techniques/concepts/piecewise.md` | Unit 07 |
| A floor or a fractional part | the same card, then `docs/techniques/floor.md` | `U11-D09` through `U11-D12`. Leave `floor` unticked. |
| A ratio of polynomials | `docs/techniques/concepts/algebra.md`, then `partial-fractions.md` | Unit 05, then 22 |
| A finite sum, or a geometric kernel inside its interval | `docs/techniques/concepts/series.md` | Unit 08, then 17. Unit 32 works `U32-D01`, fades `U32-D02`, and sets bare `U32-D04`. |
| A power of sine or cosine, or \(\tan^2 x\), or \(\sec x\) | `docs/techniques/concepts/trigonometric.md` | Unit 09 or 20, then 29 |
| A radical \(a^2-x^2\), \(a^2+x^2\), or \(x^2-a^2\) | the same card | Unit 04, then 21 |
| A rational function of sine and cosine | the same card | Unit 26. Worked `U26-D01`, faded `U26-D08`, bare `U26-D02`. |
| A numerator \(f'g-fg'\) over a square \(g^2\) | `docs/techniques/concepts/integration-by-parts.md` | Unit 27. Worked `U27-D01`, faded `U27-D04`, bare `U27-D08`. |
| A polynomial times \(e^x\), \(\sin x\), or \(\cos x\) | the same card | Unit 28. Worked `U28-D01`, faded `U28-D03`, bare `U28-D08`. |
| An inverse with no extra factor | `docs/techniques/concepts/symmetry-kings.md` | Unit 10 |
| An inverse times \(x\) | the same card, then unit 31 | Worked `U31-D01`, faded `U31-D05`, bare `U31-D07`. |
| \(e^{-x^2}\), or \((\sin x)/x\) on a half-line | `docs/techniques/concepts/gaussian-dirichlet.md` | Unit 11, then 30. Worked `U30-D01`, faded `U30-D04`, bare `U30-D06`. |
| \(e^{ax}\cos bx\) or \(e^{ax}\sin bx\) | `docs/techniques/concepts/habits.md` | Unit 32. Worked `U32-D05`, faded `U32-D07`, bare `U32-D08`. |
| \(\ln(1+x)/(1+x^2)\) on a finite interval | the same card | Unit 33. Worked `U33-D04`, faded `U33-D03`, bare `U33-D02`. |
| A half-line integral that wants the copy at \(1/x\) | the same card | Unit 33. Worked `U33-D06`, faded `U33-D07`, bare `U33-D05`. |
| A parameter on a fixed interval | `docs/techniques/concepts/algebra.md` and `docs/techniques/introduce-constant.md` | `U11-D13` through `U11-D15`. Leave `batis` unticked. |
| An endpoint that moves with the parameter, or a difference of exponentials over \(x\) | the same card | Unit 34. `U34-D07` is the substitution that needs no parameter. |

`vector-calc`, `beta`, `zeta`, `euler-mascheroni`, and `catalan` stay without an invented value. Unit 35 keeps the series it already prints. Unit 36 leaves \(G\) as \(G\).

After the learner has answered a catalog id, a solutions note may be opened under the rule in `pedagogy/README.md`. The routing lesson itself does not open one.
