# Round 3 coach

Chainlink, dichotomy, and tournament. Clocks, point values, lifelines, the attempt-shape table, and the score of a stored drill are `docs/techniques/formats.md`. This file is the session order. A formats miss still teaches that note, because `say` names one file.

## Order

1. Name the game: chainlink, dichotomy, or tournament.
2. If the integrand's catalog node is missing or `learning`, teach it from `pedagogy/round1-coach.md` and stop. The format minute starts when that node is solid, or when the learner asked for the game.
3. Read the attempt-shape row and the matching vignette under "Score a stored drill".
4. Set the drill that vignette names. The learner writes the object in that row.
5. After they answer, say the format points. The mark turn still records the drill as correct or incorrect. Quote the stored value from the unit note. Leave every pack file closed.

The dose matches `pedagogy/round2-coach.md`: worked reads the section and the vignette, faded asks for the score line from a stored value, and bare hides the value.

## Chainlink

The object is the value, then a decision: bank or continue. The clock is about 120 minutes, maximum 150. Problems arrive one at a time. A return is not allowed.

After a correct drill, the learner says bank or continue, and names the net a failure would pay. The starting nets are 1, 3, and 6. The points on the bank row are the table in `docs/techniques/formats.md`. A failed chain pays the bank value of the highest starting net already reached.

Say these three lines until they are immediate. Two solves, then a failure, pay 2. Banking those two solves pays 4. Five solves, then a failure, pay 7. Banking five pays 15. Six solves, then a failure, pay 20.

Set `U20-D04` for the decision. The stored value is \(8/15\). The vignette is the chainlink paragraph under "Score a stored drill".

Lifelines, said before they are used:

- Stop-loss, twice in the first half, before the game ends. Add one net from \(\{2, 4, 5, 7\}\) for this game only.
- Take the money and run, once in the first half, before the third attempt. Void the current problem and bank. The void is not a solve.
- This problem is dread, twice in the first half, before the third attempt. Void and continue. On the last problem of the game the void banks.
- In the second half, dread may be used three times. A void stays inside the chain and adds no solve. A skip ends the chain. There is no safety net. The 2024 illustration in the formats note scores 15.

Name a miss when a chain continues with no net named, when a failed chain is paid the bank row of the number solved, or when a second-half skip is treated as a void.

## Dichotomy

The object is the chosen problem only. The clock is about 80 minutes: three segments of 25 minutes, seven pairs in a segment, at most 21 scored problems.

A team of three rotates the solver: C, then A, then B. A team of two uses A as selector, then B as selector, and chooses the third segment. Selectors and the solver do not talk. The choice cannot be changed after it is passed.

For one learner, put `U20-D02` and `U20-D06` face down. They look at both, pass one, and cover the other before solving. Grade only the passed drill. A correct first attempt scores 6. A later correct attempt scores 2. A skip scores 0. Ten correct problems earn 8. Fifteen correct problems replace that 8 with 14. The replaced bonus and twenty-one first attempts score 140.

The stored values are \(\pi/16\) and \(2/15\). Quote a value only after that drill is the one they passed, or during a worked reading of the vignette.

Name a miss when the selector describes the integrand that was not passed, or when the covered drill is graded.

## Tournament

The object is a boxed value. The clock is about 140 minutes, four battles. Battles 1 and 2 give 2.5 minutes a problem, battle 3 gives 3, and battle 4 gives 4. Each battle is three fights of three problems. A team plays battle 1 or battle 2, then may play battle 3 and battle 4.

Say the gamble before the integrals are shown, once per battle, and only for that contestant's fight. It is one attempt on each of the three problems, and those problem points are doubled. Battle points stay 1 for being first. A gamble called after the fight has started is too late.

Set `U14-D02`. The stored value is 6. The learner boxes the line. An unboxed line spends the attempt and scores 0. In battles 1 and 2 a boxed correct answer scores 8, or 16 with a gamble declared in time. The maximum path in the formats note is 104, then 39, then 26, which is 169.

Redemption is a written answer by an eliminated team during battles 3 and 4, before a board is correct, and not while a board answer is being checked. It scores 2 in battle 3 and 1 in battle 4. It is not spoken, a gamble does not apply, and those points stay off the 169.

Name a miss when the line is unboxed, when the gamble is called after the fight starts, or when redemption is shouted.

## Closed during the lesson

A solutions note stays closed while the lesson is the format. `pedagogy/README.md` says when that note may be opened. The moment is after an answer to that id. The format lesson still uses a unit drill.
