\section*{Status}
Tags: \texttt{yang:5.3}, \texttt{yang:5.4}; techniques: \texttt{advanced-gaussian}, \texttt{dirichlet}.
Two named values do the heavy lifting: $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx=\sqrt{\pi}$ and $\int_0^{\infty}(\sin x)/x\,dx=\pi/2$. Everything else in the drill set is a substitution, a parameter derivative, or one parts step on top of those values. Yang \S\S5.3--5.4 records the contest forms. The evaluations below are the usual public arguments.

\section{Learning goals}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Quote $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx=\sqrt{\pi}/2$, then scale. Do not invent an elementary antiderivative of $e^{-x^2}$.
  \item Integrate $x e^{-x^2}$ by substituting $u=x^2$. That one is elementary.
  \item Quote $\int_0^{\infty}(\sin x)/x\,dx=\pi/2$, then change variables or integrate by parts.
  \item Refuse $\pi/2$ on a damped sine that one parts step finishes.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked Gaussian --- \texttt{U30-D01}}
Evenness gives
\[
\int_0^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx=\frac12\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2},
\]
which is the stored answer of \texttt{U30-D01}. The full line is \texttt{U30-D03}, stored value $\sqrt{\pi}$. Quoting \texttt{U30-D03} on the half-line is the miss. The worked dose stops at the half-line.

One evaluation of the square, optional here, is the polar computation
\[
\Bigl(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx\Bigr)^2=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{\infty}e^{-r^2}r\,dr\,d\theta=\pi.
\]
The positive square root of that value is the stored full-line value $\sqrt{\pi}$. Vector calculus is not required for Round 1.

For $a>0$, the substitution $u=x\sqrt{a}$ yields $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-a x^2}\,dx=\frac12\sqrt{\pi/a}$. \texttt{U35-D01} is the case $a=2$. That drill lives in unit 35.

\section{Faded --- \texttt{U30-D04}}
\[
\int_0^{\infty}x^2 e^{-x^2}\,dx.
\]
The blank is the parameter. Differentiate $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-a x^2}\,dx$ in $a$, then set $a=1$. The differentiation is the Leibniz rule from unit 34, taken as a limit of compact intervals. The stored answer is $\sqrt{\pi}/4$.

\section{Worked Dirichlet --- \texttt{U30-D05}}
For $a>0$ set $I(a)=\int_0^{\infty}e^{-ax}(\sin x)/x\,dx$. Differentiating in $a$ under the integral gives
\[
I'(a)=-\int_0^{\infty}e^{-ax}\sin x\,dx=-\frac{1}{a^2+1}.
\]
$I(a)\to 0$ as $a\to\infty$, so $I(a)=\pi/2-\arctan a$. The limit $a\to 0^+$ is $\pi/2$. That is the stored answer of \texttt{U30-D05}. The family $I(a)$ is the argument. It is not a second drill id. The switch of derivative and integral is a limit; unit 34 states the compact case.

\section{Bare --- \texttt{U30-D06}}
Set
\[
\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\sin^2 x}{x^2}\,dx
\]
and show nothing else. The bare dose hides the parts step. After a line is written, uncover it. Parts with $u=\sin^2 x$ and $dv=x^{-2}\,dx$ kills the boundary term in the limit and leaves $\int_0^{\infty}\sin(2x)/x\,dx$. The substitution $u=2x$ turns that into \texttt{U30-D05}. The stored answer is $\pi/2$. The same number as \texttt{U30-D05} is not a reason to skip the parts step.

\section{Refusal --- elementary, or one parts step}
\texttt{U30-D02} is
\[
\int_0^{\infty}x e^{-x^2}\,dx=\frac12.
\]
The extra factor of $x$ makes the substitution $u=x^2$ elementary. Refuse an elementary antiderivative of $e^{-x^2}$ in its place. The named Gaussian stays \texttt{U30-D01}.

\texttt{U30-D07} and \texttt{U30-D08} are $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-x}\sin x\,dx$ and the cosine partner. Each stores $1/2$. Refuse $\pi/2$. Both are one-line parts, or the imaginary and real parts of $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-x}e^{ix}\,dx$ in unit 32, where the same numbers are \texttt{U32-D06} and \texttt{U32-D05}. They do not use the Dirichlet value.

\section{Dose}
Worked reads \texttt{U30-D01}, names \texttt{U30-D03} as the full line, then reads \texttt{U30-D05} when the node is \texttt{dirichlet}. Faded gives only the parameter blank on \texttt{U30-D04}. Bare sets \texttt{U30-D06}. An elementary antiderivative of $e^{-x^2}$ opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U30-D02}. The value $\pi/2$ on a damped sine opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U30-D07}, then \texttt{U30-D08}.
