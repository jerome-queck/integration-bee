\section{Worked example --- \texttt{U17-D01} (\texttt{yang:2.8})}
\[
\int_{0}^{1/2}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^{n}\,dx=\int_{0}^{1/2}\frac{dx}{1-x}.
\]
Geometric sum $\Rightarrow\ln 2$.
