\section*{Status}
Tags: \texttt{yang:6.8}; techniques: \texttt{modified-famous}.
A famous integral with one constant changed is still that integral after a substitution. Yang \S6.8 collects those tweaks. Scaling a Gaussian or a Dirichlet integral is the Gaussian card, \texttt{docs/techniques/concepts/gaussian-dirichlet.md}. The drills below write that substitution out, then keep the series and $\ln(\sin x)$ that the card leaves in this file. The route is \texttt{docs/techniques/concepts/habits.md}. None of these drills needs a new constant invented on the spot.
\section{Official anchors}
\begin{itemize}
\item Unit 30 for the Gaussian and $\int_0^{\infty}(\sin x)/x\,dx=\pi/2$.
\item The Basel value $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/n^2=\pi^2/6$, and the alternating partner $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}/n^2=\pi^2/12$.
\item Yang Topics Guide 2nd Ed \S6.8 Modified Famous Integrals. Syllabus \S2 names zeta.
\end{itemize}
\section{Learning goals}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Scale the limit and the constant in the exponent or the argument. Write the substitution.
  \item Expand $1/(1\pm x)$ and integrate $x^n\ln x$ termwise when the famous value is a zeta number.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scale the Gaussian --- \texttt{U35-D01} (\texttt{modified-famous})}
\[
\int_0^{\infty}e^{-2x^2}\,dx.
\]
\texttt{U30-D01} stores $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-u^2}\,du=\sqrt{\pi}/2$. Set $u=x\sqrt{2}$, so $dx=du/\sqrt{2}$:
\[
\int_0^{\infty}e^{-u^2}\,\frac{du}{\sqrt{2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2\sqrt{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{4}.
\]
That is the stored value. The same substitution with $u=3x$ is a scale, not a drill:
\[
\int_0^{\infty}e^{-9x^2}\,dx=\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{6}.
\]
SymPy checks $\sqrt{\pi}/6$.

\texttt{U35-D03} is $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-3x}\,dx=1/3$. The exponent is linear, so the value is the elementary integral $1/a$ at $a=3$.

\section{Scale the Dirichlet integral --- \texttt{U35-D02}}
\[
\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\sin 2x}{x}\,dx.
\]
\texttt{U30-D05} stores $\int_0^{\infty}(\sin u)/u\,du=\pi/2$. Set $u=2x$, so $dx=du/2$ and
\[
\frac{\sin 2x}{x}\,dx=\frac{\sin u}{u/2}\cdot\frac{du}{2}=\frac{\sin u}{u}\,du.
\]
The integral equals \texttt{U30-D05}, value $\pi/2$. That is the stored value. The factor $2$ cancels.

The Gaussian card already records the same cancellation for a general $b>0$. In particular
\[
\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\sin 3x}{x}\,dx=\frac\pi2.
\]
SymPy returns $\pi/2$. That line is a scale of \texttt{U30-D05}, and it is not a drill id.

\section{Series --- \texttt{U35-D04} and \texttt{U35-D05}}
On $[0,1)$,
\[
\frac{1}{1-x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n,\qquad \int_0^1 x^n\ln x\,dx=-\frac{1}{(n+1)^2}.
\]
So
\[
\int_0^1\frac{\ln x}{1-x}\,dx=-\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{m^2}=-\frac{\pi^2}{6}.
\]
The alternating expansion of $1/(1+x)$ produces
\[
\int_0^1\frac{\ln x}{1+x}\,dx=-\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{m-1}}{m^2}=-\frac{\pi^2}{12}.
\]
Those are the stored answers. They are the Basel values this unit already prints. A further zeta value is not part of the drill set. The endpoint $x=1$ in \texttt{U35-D04} is improper. The series of positive terms and the integral agree as improper integrals. \texttt{U35-D08} is the elementary cousin $\int_0^1 -\ln x\,dx=1$.

\section{Logarithm of sine --- \texttt{U35-D06}}
\[
I=\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin x)\,dx.
\]
The reflection $x\mapsto\pi/2-x$ shows $I=\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\cos x)\,dx$, so
\[
2I=\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin x\cos x)\,dx=\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin 2x)\,dx-\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln 2\,dx.
\]
The second integral is $(\pi/2)\ln 2$. For the first, $v=2x$ gives $\frac12\int_0^{\pi}\ln(\sin v)\,dv=\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin v)\,dv=I$, because $\sin(\pi-v)=\sin v$. Therefore $2I=I-(\pi/2)\ln 2$, and $I=-(\pi/2)\ln 2$. \texttt{U36-D02} is the same integral.

\texttt{U35-D07} is $(e^{-x}-e^{-2x})/x$ on $[0,\infty)$, stored value $\ln 2$. That is \texttt{U34-D01}. Use the parameter argument there. Do not start a series.
