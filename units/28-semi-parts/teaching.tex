\section*{Status}
Tags: \texttt{yang:5.1}; techniques: \texttt{semi-parts}.
Integration by parts still proves these antiderivatives. The speed move is to write the shape of the answer and match coefficients, or to run the tabular form of parts. Yang \S5.1 calls the shortcut semi-parts. The derivative check is the source of truth.

\section{Learning goals}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item For $p(x)e^x$, $p(x)\sin x$, or $p(x)\cos x$ with $p$ a polynomial, assume an answer of the same shape and differentiate.
  \item For a longer polynomial, tabulate: differentiate one column, integrate the other, and alternate signs.
  \item Refuse the ansatz when the integrand is a logarithm, or when sine and exponential return together.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked coefficients --- \texttt{U28-D01}}
\[
\int x e^x\,dx.
\]
Assume $(ax+b)e^x$. Differentiate:
\[
ae^x+(ax+b)e^x=(ax+a+b)e^x.
\]
Match $a=1$ and $a+b=0$, so $b=-1$. The antiderivative is $(x-1)e^x$. On $[0,1]$ that is $1$, the stored answer of \texttt{U28-D07}.

\texttt{U28-D02} stores $-x\cos x+\sin x$. Differentiate that expression. It returns $x\sin x$. The coefficient match is the same shape with sine and cosine swapped.

\section{Faded --- \texttt{U28-D03}}
\[
\int x\cos x\,dx.
\]
Assume $(ax+b)\sin x+(cx+d)\cos x$. The blank is the four coefficients. Differentiate, match $x\cos x$, and read $a=1$, $b=0$, $c=0$, $d=1$. The stored antiderivative is $x\sin x+\cos x$.

\section{Bare --- \texttt{U28-D08}}
Set
\[
\int x^2\sin x\,dx
\]
and show nothing else. The bare dose hides the table. After a line is written, uncover the signs $+,-,+$ on the pairs
\[
x^2(-\cos x)-(2x)(-\sin x)+2(\cos x)=-x^2\cos x+2x\sin x+2\cos x.
\]
That is the stored antiderivative.

The tabular integral of $x^3 e^x$ is \texttt{U23-D06} in unit 23. It is a different polynomial. Do not mark it as \texttt{U28-D08}.

\section{Refusal --- the ansatz does not fit}
\texttt{U28-D06} is $\int\ln x\,dx$. The shape $(ax+b)e^x$ does not match a logarithm. One parts step, with $u=\ln x$ and $dv=dx$, stores $x\ln x-x$. Refuse the exponential table.

\texttt{U28-D04} is $\int x\ln x\,dx$. The same refusal. Parts with $u=\ln x$ stores
\[
\frac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\frac{x^2}{4}.
\]

\texttt{U28-D05} is $\int_0^{\pi}e^x\sin x\,dx=(1+e^{\pi})/2$. Sine and exponential together return after two parts steps. A single polynomial in front of $e^x$ does not close the equation. Refuse stopping after one undetermined polynomial. Call the integral $I$, parts twice, and solve.

\section{Dose}
Worked reads \texttt{U28-D01}, then the faded coefficients on \texttt{U28-D03}, then sets \texttt{U28-D08}. Faded gives only those four blanks. Bare sets \texttt{U28-D08}. A logarithm, or a cyclic pair, opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U28-D06}, then \texttt{U28-D05}. \texttt{U28-D07} is the definite check of the worked antiderivative, value $1$.
