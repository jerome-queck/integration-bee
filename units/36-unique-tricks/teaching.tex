Yang \S6.9 is a drawer of leftovers. In this drill set every integral is one of three things: a parameter integral from unit 34, a substitution $u=\ln x$, or Catalan's constant left as Catalan's constant. Stop at the justified value $G$; do not guess an elementary replacement. The route is \texttt{docs/techniques/concepts/habits.md}. This unit hosts the ready \texttt{catalan} recognition lesson, with card \texttt{docs/techniques/concepts/catalan.md}. It is optional and has no Round 1 gate. Ready describes the lesson, not a learner's mastery.
\section{Official anchors}
\begin{itemize}
\item Unit 34 for $\int_0^1 (x^b-x^a)/\ln x\,dx=\ln((b+1)/(a+1))$.
\item Unit 35 for $\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin x)\,dx=-(\pi/2)\ln 2$.
\item Catalan's constant $G=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^k/(2k+1)^2$. Syllabus \S2 lists it. Yang Topics Guide 2nd Ed \S6.9 Unique Tricks.
\end{itemize}
\section{Learning goals}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Recognise $(x^b-x^a)/\ln x$ and integrate the geometric parameter. Do not series-expand $\ln x$ in the denominator.
  \item Leave $G$ as $G$. Do not replace it by a rational number or a logarithm.
  \item For $1/(x\ln x)$, substitute $u=\ln x$. Refuse that antiderivative when the numerator is already $\ln x$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked parameter --- \texttt{U36-D01}}
\[
\int_0^1\frac{x-1}{\ln x}\,dx=\ln 2.
\]
For $a>-1$, $\int_0^1 x^a\,dx=1/(a+1)$. Integrate in $a$ from 0 to 1:
\[
\int_0^1\frac{x-1}{\ln x}\,dx=\ln\frac{1+1}{0+1}=\ln 2.
\]
That is the stored answer. The worked dose stops there. The improper endpoint is the same limit used in unit 34.

\section{Faded parameter --- \texttt{U36-D07}}
\[
\int_0^1\frac{x^2-1}{\ln x}\,dx.
\]
The blank is the upper limit of $a$. It is $2$, not $1$. For $a>-1$, $\int_0^1 x^a\,dx=1/(a+1)$. Integrate in $a$ from $0$ to $2$:
\[
\ln(2+1)-\ln(0+1)=\ln 3.
\]
After that limit is written, uncover $\ln 3$. Refuse $\ln 2$. This integral is \texttt{U34-D04}.

The same formula with the upper limit $4$ is a fence on \texttt{docs/techniques/concepts/habits.md}, not a drill:
\[
\int_0^1\frac{x^4-1}{\ln x}\,dx=\ln 5.
\]
SymPy checks $\ln 5$. The parameter from $0$ to $3$ is already stored as \texttt{U11-D15}, value $\ln 4$. The \texttt{dutis} lesson in unit 34 links that fixed-interval argument. The fence $\ln 5$ is a parameter value, not a replacement for $G$.

\section{Worked constant --- \texttt{U36-D03}}
\[
\int_0^1\frac{\arctan x}{x}\,dx=G.
\]
On $[0,1)$, $\arctan x=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^k x^{2k+1}/(2k+1)$, so
\[
\frac{\arctan x}{x}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(-1)^k\frac{x^{2k}}{2k+1}.
\]
To justify the endpoint, integrate the finite geometric expansion of $1/(1+x^2)$. Truncating the arctangent series at $k=N$ leaves a remainder of absolute value at most $x^{2N+3}/(2N+3)$ for $0\leq x\leq1$. After division by $x$ and integration, the error is at most $1/(2N+3)^2$, which tends to zero. Each polynomial term contributes $(-1)^k/(2k+1)^2$. The resulting sum is the definition of $G$. The integrand extends continuously through $x=0$ by the value $1$. The stored answer is the constant $G$, written \texttt{Catalan} in the drill feed. The worked dose stops there.

\section{Faded term --- \texttt{U36-D03}}
The blank is the general term $(-1)^k/(2k+1)^2$. Then ask why the integrated error $1/(2N+3)^2$ tends to zero. There is no second stored drill for this constant. After the term is written, uncover $G$.

\section{Bare --- \texttt{U36-D03}}
Set
\[
\int_0^1\frac{\arctan x}{x}\,dx
\]
and show nothing else. The bare dose hides the series. After a line is written, uncover $G$.

\section{Refusal --- $G$ stays $G$}
A guessed logarithm or a rational in place of $G$ is the miss. The stored answer of \texttt{U36-D03} is $G$. This stopping rule does not claim that an alternative exact form has been proved impossible; such a replacement would need its own justified identity.

\section{Worked logarithm --- \texttt{U36-D04}}
\[
\int\frac{dx}{x\ln x}=\ln|\ln x|+C.
\]
Let $u=\ln x$, so $du=dx/x$. The worked dose stops there.

\section{Faded --- \texttt{U36-D05}}
On $[2,4]$,
\[
\int_2^4\frac{dx}{x\ln x}.
\]
The blank is the subtraction $\ln(\ln 4)-\ln(\ln 2)$. Because $\ln 4=2\ln 2$,
\[
\ln(2\ln 2)-\ln(\ln 2)=\ln 2.
\]
After that subtraction is written, uncover $\ln 2$. That is the stored value.

\section{Bare --- \texttt{U36-D06}}
Set
\[
\int_e^{e^e}\frac{dx}{x\ln x}
\]
and show nothing else. The bare dose hides the endpoints. After a line is written, uncover
\[
\bigl[\ln|\ln x|\bigr]_e^{e^e}=\ln\bigl(\ln(e^e)\bigr)-\ln(\ln e)=\ln e-\ln 1=1.
\]
That is the stored value.

\section{Refusal --- the numerator is already $\ln x$}
\texttt{U36-D08} is $\int(\ln x)/x\,dx$. The same $u=\ln x$ gives $\int u\,du=\frac12(\ln x)^2$, the stored antiderivative. Refuse $\ln|\ln x|$, the answer of \texttt{U36-D04}.

\section{Already taught --- \texttt{U36-D02}}
\[
\int_0^{\pi/2}\ln(\sin x)\,dx=-\frac\pi2\ln 2
\]
is \texttt{U35-D06}. Use the double-angle argument in that note. Calling it a unique trick does not change the proof. The stored value is not $G$.

\section{Dose}
For a parameter quotient, worked reads \texttt{U36-D01}, then the faded upper limit on \texttt{U36-D07}. Faded gives only that limit. A copied $\ln 2$ opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U36-D07}. The value $\ln 5$ is the fence on \texttt{docs/techniques/concepts/habits.md}, not a drill. The \texttt{dutis} lesson is hosted in unit 34.

For $1/(x\ln x)$, worked reads \texttt{U36-D04}, then the faded subtraction on \texttt{U36-D05}, then sets \texttt{U36-D06}. Faded gives only that subtraction. Bare sets \texttt{U36-D06}. Writing $\ln|\ln x|$ for $\int(\ln x)/x\,dx$ opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U36-D08}.

For Catalan's constant, worked reads the series for \texttt{U36-D03} and stops at $G$. Faded gives only the general term and asks for the error limit. Bare sets \texttt{U36-D03}. A guessed logarithm or a rational opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U36-D03}. These three doses use one stored drill id, whose practice credit stays on \texttt{unique-tricks}. The optional \texttt{catalan} node has no automatic drill queue or automatic credit from that host-unit answer. The oral recognition explanation supplements the existing drill and adds no new machine id.

\texttt{U36-D02} is \texttt{U35-D06}. Use that note. The stored value is $-(\pi/2)\ln 2$.

\section*{On a miss}
Teach this file. If the answer is $G$, say so and stop. If the denominator is $\ln x$, build the parameter or the substitution $u=\ln x$. If $\ln 2$ was written for $(x^2-1)/\ln x$, set \texttt{U36-D07}. If $\ln|\ln x|$ was written for $(\ln x)/x$, set \texttt{U36-D08}. For a Catalan recognition miss, reopen the alternating odd-square term and the remainder bound. Do not invent an answer that is not in \texttt{drills.md}.
