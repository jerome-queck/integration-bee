\section*{Status}
Tags: \texttt{yang:4.6}; techniques: \texttt{reverse-quotient}.
The quotient rule says $(f/g)'=(f'g-fg')/g^2$. Integrating the right-hand side returns $f/g$. Yang \S4.6 collects contest shapes of that right-hand side. Unit 07 is the product-rule twin. You do not need a new calculus fact.

\section{Learning goals}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Read a numerator $f'g-fg'$ and a denominator $g^2$ as $(f/g)'$.
  \item Guess $f/g$ when the denominator is a square, then differentiate to check.
  \item Refuse a logarithmic derivative, and refuse the product rule, when the minus sign is the whole method.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked --- \texttt{U27-D01}}
\[
\int\frac{x\cos x-\sin x}{x^2}\,dx.
\]
Here $f=\sin x$ and $g=x$. Then $f'g-fg'=x\cos x-\sin x$ and $g^2=x^2$. The antiderivative is $\sin x/x$. That is the stored answer.

The worked dose stops there. The next two drills are the faded blank and the bare integral.

\section{Faded --- \texttt{U27-D04}}
\[
\int\frac{\sin x-x\cos x}{\sin^2 x}\,dx.
\]
The denominator is a square, so name $g$ before you name $f$. The blank is the pair. Here $g=\sin x$ and $f=x$. Differentiate $x/\sin x$. The numerator matches, and the stored antiderivative is $x/\sin x$.

\texttt{U27-D07} is the same blank with another pair: $f=\tan x$ and $g=x$. The stored antiderivative is $\tan x/x$.

\section{Bare --- \texttt{U27-D08}}
Set
\[
\int\frac{x^2\cos x-2x\sin x}{x^4}\,dx
\]
and show nothing else. The bare dose hides $f$ and $g$. After a line is written, uncover $f=\sin x$, $g=x^2$, and the stored antiderivative $\sin x/x^2$.

\section{Same rule on an exponential}
\texttt{U27-D02} is $e^x(x-1)/x^2$. Take $f=e^x$ and $g=x$:
\[
f'g-fg'=e^x\cdot x-e^x\cdot 1=e^x(x-1).
\]
The stored antiderivative is $e^x/x$. \texttt{U27-D06} uses $g=x+1$ and stores $e^x/(x+1)$.

\section{Refusal --- not a logarithmic derivative}
\texttt{U27-D03} is $(1-\ln x)/x^2$. The numerator is $f'g-fg'$ with $f=\ln x$ and $g=x$. It is not $f'$ alone. Unit 24 integrates $f'/f$. Refuse that reading. The stored antiderivative is $\ln x/x$.

\texttt{U27-D05} is $(\ln x-1)/x^2$, the sign flip of \texttt{U27-D03}. The stored antiderivative is $-\ln x/x$. Copying the answer of \texttt{U27-D03} is the miss.

A plus in the numerator, $f'g+fg'$, is the product rule, unit 07. The minus is this unit. \texttt{U27-D02} carries the minus. Do not send it to the product.

\section{Dose}
Worked reads \texttt{U27-D01}, then the faded blank on \texttt{U27-D04}, then sets \texttt{U27-D08}. Faded gives only that blank. Bare sets \texttt{U27-D08} and hides the pair. A logarithmic-derivative attempt, or a copied sign, opens the refusal and sets \texttt{U27-D03}, then \texttt{U27-D05}.

\section*{On a miss}
Teach this file. Name $f$ and $g$, then differentiate $f/g$. If the numerator has no minus term, refuse this unit and open unit 24. Do not invent an answer that is not in \texttt{drills.md}.
