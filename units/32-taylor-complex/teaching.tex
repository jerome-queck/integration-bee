A geometric series on $[0,1)$ and Euler's formula $e^{ix}=\cos x+i\sin x$ are standard. Yang \S\S6.2--6.3 packages them as contest moves. Unit 08 is the first geometric integral. This unit says when the closed form is faster than the series, and when the complex exponential replaces two parts steps.

\section{Learning goals}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Integrate a geometric series term by term on $[0,1)$, then take the endpoint as a limit.
  \item If a closed antiderivative is one substitution, use it. The series explains the number. It is not always the fastest path.
  \item Replace $e^{ax}\cos bx$ by the real part of $e^{(a+ib)x}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked series --- \texttt{U32-D01}}
\[
\int_0^1\frac{dx}{1+x}=\ln 2.
\]
The antiderivative is the fast path. The series path is the reason the value is the alternating harmonic number:
\[
\frac{1}{1+x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n x^n,\qquad 0\le x<1,
\]
and $\int_0^1 x^n\,dx=1/(n+1)$, so the integral is $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n/(n+1)=\ln 2$. At $x=1$ the series converges, and the integral of the limit matches the limit of the integrals.

\texttt{U32-D02} is a polynomial, not an infinite series. Integrate termwise:
\[
\int_0^1\Bigl(1-x^2+\frac{x^4}{2}-\frac{x^6}{6}\Bigr)dx=1-\frac13+\frac{1}{10}-\frac{1}{42}=\frac{26}{35}.
\]
\texttt{U32-D04} is $\int_0^1 x^3\,dx=1/4$. \texttt{U32-D03} is $\int x e^{x^2}\,dx=\frac12 e^{x^2}$, a substitution. A series for $e^{x^2}$ would be the long way around.

\section{Worked complex exponential --- \texttt{U32-D07}}
\[
\int e^x\cos x\,dx.
\]
$e^x\cos x$ is the real part of $e^x e^{ix}=e^{(1+i)x}$.
\[
\int e^{(1+i)x}\,dx=\frac{e^{(1+i)x}}{1+i}=\frac{1-i}{2}\,e^{(1+i)x}.
\]
The real part is $\frac12 e^x(\cos x+\sin x)$, the stored answer. The imaginary part is \texttt{U32-D08}, $\frac12 e^x(\sin x-\cos x)$.

On $[0,\infty)$, \texttt{U32-D05} is $\int_0^{\infty}e^{-x}\cos x\,dx=1/2$ and \texttt{U32-D06} is the sine partner, also $1/2$. Both are
\[
\mathrm{Re}\int_0^{\infty}e^{-(1-i)x}\,dx\quad\text{or its imaginary part.}
\]
The same two values are \texttt{U30-D08} and \texttt{U30-D07}.
